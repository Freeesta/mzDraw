# Labels that do not crowd, built

a bond stops short of what stands on its side of an element symbol, not only of the symbol: the charge at the upper right (`O−` and `H3N+` with a bond leaving to the right), the hydrogens with their count, the mass number (`Painter::clear` in `scene.rs`, on top of the label margin of the style; a bond never loses more than 45 % of its length at one end, and one that leaves downwards passes under a raised charge unshortened). Hydrogens written on the left stand before the mass number (`H3 13C`; they were drawn over it). Covered by `tests/crowding.rs`; one line of one golden SVG moved (a bond end in front of a charge). A second pass on a sheet of 28 synthetic structures (`tests/data/flaw_sheet.smi`: ring NH, charged ring atoms, halides, silicon, wedges to labels, radicals, acids) fixed the three flaws seen first: a bond keeps clear of a symbol of two letters (`Cl`, `Br`, `Si` are wider than the label margin allows for); with bonds on both sides of a symbol the hydrogens stand under it, or over it when the bonds leave downwards (the NH of a ring was written `NH` into its ring bond), a count at the lower right of the H and the charge at the upper right of the symbol as before; the wide end of a solid or hashed wedge stops half a wedge width earlier in front of a label. Three golden SVGs moved in the lines these rules touch. Not done here: the positions of the atoms themselves (the 2D layout is the one of `mzlab-mol`, D1), charges moved to another corner when the upper right is taken, abbreviation labels.
