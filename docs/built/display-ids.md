# Atom and bond ids in the picture, built (rdkit-compare 4)

Found by the RDKit comparison (`docs/study/rdkit-compare.md` matrix #36; RDKit's `addAtomIndices`, `addBondIndices`): an agent names atoms and bonds by their ids in every operation of `draw_edit/1`, and could not see them. The setting `display.ids` draws them: `off` (default), `atoms`, `bonds`, `both`. These are the ids of the document, the numbers `atom.set`, `bond.set`, `remove` and the others take (not 0-based indices, which change with a deletion).

**Setting.** `--set display.ids=<word>` (the replay request `"set"`), key `ids` of the settings file (`mzdraw-settings/1`, written only when not `off`), `Editor::get("display.ids")`, the word list in `settings::schema_json` (`display.ids`), a schema row in the section `set` (`edit-schema.json` and the table of `docs/mzdraw.md` re-blessed). A word that is none is `error.draw2.styleValue` and changes nothing. Code: `settings::Ids`, `Style::ids`, `Metrics::ids`.

**Drawing.** `scene::Painter::ids`, after the atoms, in the small size and the muted ink: so the canvas, the SVG, the PDF and the PNG (the page draws the SVG) all show them. Each id goes where it is farthest from what is drawn near it (`scene::id_spot`): an atom's id on one of 16 places on a circle round the atom (0.42 bond lengths, or past its label); a bond's id on either side of its middle (past the second and third lines of a double or triple bond). "Near" is the atoms within two bonds, three points along each of their bonds, the extent of their labels, their lone pairs and the ids already placed within two bond lengths. Every lookup is indexed (positions by id, the bonds of each atom, the placed ids in a grid of cells one bond length wide) and the label test and the lone pairs of each atom are taken once per atom, so the work the ids add depends on the bonds round each id, not on the size of the drawing (test `the_work_grows_with_the_drawing_not_with_its_square`: the picture with ids less the picture without, for 40 and 160 structures, at most six times as much for four times the structures; the least CPU time of runs taken in turns, `tests/support/timing.rs`). The painter's own label test (`scene::labelled`, through `bonds_of`) scans the bonds of the drawing per atom with or without ids: that cost is its queued follow-up, not part of this slice. The first of equal places wins, so the same drawing gives the same picture, and an id keeps its place when something is added more than two bond lengths away.

**Page.** A row of the Preferences (group Display, `prefs::WORDS`), with its texts in `web/lang/mzdraw.js` (`draw2.prefs.display.ids.*`); Reset of the group puts it back to `off`. Screenshots at deviceScaleFactor 2: `docs/shots/rdkit-compare-4-display-ids-prefs.png` (the panel) and `-canvas.png` (the canvas) are written by the e2e step under those names (`MZDRAW_SHOTS=DIR node tests/e2e.mjs`); `-svg.png` is the SVG the CLI writes for `{"import":"c1ccc(cc1)C(=O)[NH3+]","set":["display.ids=both"],"out":"svg"}`, put in a page with `page.setContent` and taken with `locator("svg").screenshot()` in Chromium at deviceScaleFactor 2.

Tests: `crates/mzdraw/tests/display_ids.rs` (off by default and each word, in the SVG and the PDF; each id nearer its own atom or bond than any other, clear of every line, of every label and of the other ids, on four structures with a ring, labels, a charge and multiple bonds; stable: the same places when drawn twice and when a structure is added far away; the work linear in the drawing; the settings file); `crates/mzdraw/tests/prefs.rs` (the reset list of the group Display has the new row); golden cases `tests/data/log/display-ids.json` (both, atoms with labels, a map number and a charge, bonds of a ring, ids named by a log, a refused word), wasm == native in `tests/parity.mjs`; e2e step "display ids (rdkit-compare 4)" (keyboard, touch, pen; the page equals the golden, Reset hides them).

Not built: ids drawn for objects (arrows, texts: their ids are in `digest`); a choice of colour or size for the ids; ids in CDXML (no such field there).
