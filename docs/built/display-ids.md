# Atom and bond ids in the picture, built (rdkit-compare 4)

Found by the RDKit comparison (`docs/study/rdkit-compare.md` matrix #36; RDKit's `addAtomIndices`, `addBondIndices`): an agent names atoms and bonds by their ids in every operation of `draw_edit/1`, and could not see them. The setting `display.ids` draws them: `off` (default), `atoms`, `bonds`, `both`. These are the ids of the document, the numbers `atom.set`, `bond.set`, `remove` and the others take (not 0-based indices, which change with a deletion).

**Setting.** `--set display.ids=<word>` (the replay request `"set"`), key `ids` of the settings file (`mzdraw-settings/1`, written only when not `off`), `Editor::get("display.ids")`, the word list in `settings::schema_json` (`display.ids`), a schema row in the section `set` (`edit-schema.json` and the table of `docs/mzdraw.md` re-blessed). A word that is none is `error.draw2.styleValue` and changes nothing. Code: `settings::Ids`, `Style::ids`, `Metrics::ids`.

**Drawing.** `scene::Painter::ids`, after the atoms, in the small size and the muted ink: so the canvas, the SVG, the PDF and the PNG (the page draws the SVG) all show them. Each id goes where it is farthest from what is drawn near it (`scene::id_spot`): an atom's id on one of 16 places on a circle round the atom (0.42 bond lengths, or past its label); a bond's id on either side of its middle (past the second and third lines of a double or triple bond). "Near" is the atoms within two bonds, three points along each of their bonds, the extent of their labels, their lone pairs and the ids already placed, so the work per id does not grow with the drawing; the first of equal places wins, so the same drawing gives the same picture.

**Page.** A row of the Preferences (group Display, `prefs::WORDS`), with its texts in `web/lang/mzdraw.js` (`draw2.prefs.display.ids.*`); Reset of the group puts it back to `off`. Screenshots at deviceScaleFactor 2: `docs/shots/rdkit-compare-4-display-ids-prefs.png` (the panel), `-canvas.png` (the canvas), `-svg.png` (an SVG written by the CLI, drawn by Chromium).

Tests: `crates/mzdraw/tests/display_ids.rs` (off by default and each word; each id nearer its own atom or bond than any other, clear of every line and of the other ids, the same places twice, on four structures with a ring, labels, a charge and multiple bonds; the PDF; the settings file); `crates/mzdraw/tests/prefs.rs` (the reset list of the group Display has the new row); golden cases `tests/data/log/display-ids.json` (both, atoms with labels, a map number and a charge, bonds of a ring, ids named by a log, a refused word), wasm == native in `tests/parity.mjs`; e2e step "display ids (rdkit-compare 4)" (keyboard, touch, pen; the page equals the golden, Reset hides them).

Not built: ids drawn for objects (arrows, texts: their ids are in `digest`); a choice of colour or size for the ids; ids in CDXML (no such field there).
