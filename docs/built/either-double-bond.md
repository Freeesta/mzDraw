# "Either" double bonds: an unknown E/Z written and read back, built (rdkit-compare 2)

Found by the RDKit comparison (`docs/study/rdkit-compare.md` F3): a double bond whose E/Z is not given (the SMILES `CC=CC`) was drawn with a geometry and written as a plain double bond, so a reader that takes E/Z from 2D coordinates (RDKit, `mzlab-mol`) read a defined E or Z; and a molfile double bond marked "either" (bond stereo 3) lost its mark on import.

**The field.** A double bond may carry `"ez": "either"` in `mzdraw/1` (kept in the unknown-field list of the bond, as `pos` and `k` are: a document without it has the same bytes, an older reader keeps it). `Bond::either()` reads it (double bonds only).

**The operation.** No new field of `draw_edit/1`: `bond.set` with `s: 3` on a double bond sets "either" (3 is the wavy mark, the "either" of a molfile), `s: 0` takes it away; a wedge mark (`s` 1, 2) on a double bond is still refused (`error.draw2.bond`); a new order other than 2 takes "either" away and it does not come back with the order. Inverse: `SetBond` (the whole bond), in the property test of `tests/ops.rs` (its random `bond.set` already draws `s` 0..3 on bonds of every order). `diff` says the mark (`bond.set s 3` or `s 0`), and a pasted or imported structure carries it (`editor::place_ops`). The schema row of `s` says it (`"out":"schema"`, re-blessed `edit-schema.json`; the table of `docs/mzdraw.md` re-blessed).

**Import.** A record's double bonds that can carry E/Z and have none are marked: `io::with_unknown_double_bonds` adds an `unknown` element to each, the record writes them as "either", and the marks are read off its bond lines with the wedges. "Can carry E/Z": in no ring smaller than 8 atoms, no cumulated double bond at an end, each end with one other neighbour or two of different constitution (`classes`: element, charge, mass number, hydrogens, radicals, bonds, refined by the neighbours). Substituents that differ only through stereo elsewhere are not seen (no mark: the drawing says what it said before). This is not `Mol::stereo_sites` of `mzlab-mol`, which tries assignments of the other sites and did not finish on forty pieces with ring centres (test `many_pieces_with_ring_centres_are_marked_without_a_search_of_the_sites`); on 14 SMILES it agrees with it (test `an_import_marks_exactly_...`). A molfile V2000 bond stereo 3 and V3000 `CFG=2` on a double bond, and CDXML `Display="Wavy"` on a double bond, come in as the record's `unknown` double bond and so as "either".

**Out.** V2000 molfile, SDF, RXN: bond stereo 3 (`Document::mark_wedges`); V3000: `CFG=2` (`formats.rs` `v3000`); CDXML: `Order="2" Display="Wavy"` (the convention RDKit's CDXML writer uses), except on a bond with a display kind of its own (one `Display` per bond: the kind stays, the mark is listed as lost); SMILES: no `/` `\` (the record's double bond is `unknown`), read back as "either". KET and CML have no place for it: the loss manifest row `bond.either` (molfile, V3000, SMILES, SDF, CDXML, RXN: `drawing`, a round trip decides; KET, CML: `no`; `docs/mzdraw-formats.md` re-blessed). The drawing is unchanged: an "either" double bond is drawn as a plain double bond (RDKit's default).

Evidence: RDKit reads the polyene of `tools/rdkit_compare/set.tsv` back with no E/Z from our molfile, SMILES and CDXML (`tools/rdkit_compare/reference.json` regenerated); RDKit's own CDXML reader takes E/Z from the coordinates even under `Display="Wavy"`, so that file is checked by our reader only.

Tests: `crates/mzdraw/tests/either.rs` (which bonds are marked, against `Mol::stereo_sites`; every file written and read back; the files that cannot hold it; the operation with its exact undo; the diff; forty pieces; a mark no file keeps); `crates/mzdraw/tests/loss.rs` (the new row); golden cases `tests/data/log/either.json` (wasm == native in `tests/parity.mjs`).

Not built: a visible mark on the canvas (a crossed or wavy-adjacent double bond, an option); the "either" mark by the bond tool or the context menu of the page (the CLI route is `bond.set s 3`); KET (`stereo` 3 of a bond) and CML; E/Z that differs only through stereo elsewhere.
