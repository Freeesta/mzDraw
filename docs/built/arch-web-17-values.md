# Calculated values: formula, masses, m/z of adducts (arch-web-17), built

Per connected structure and for the selection: Hill formula, elemental composition, charge, monoisotopic mass, average mass and the m/z of the adducts chosen in `display.adducts`. Shown in the side panel ("Calculated values", under "What is drawn"), optionally drawn under each structure (`display.values`), and written by the replay request field `values` as `values/1`. (Code: `crates/mzdraw/src/values.rs`; the engine call `crates/mzdraw-wasm/src/glue.rs` `measure`; editor `Editor::refresh_values` / `values_json`; painter `scene.rs` `captions`; exports `svg.rs`, `pdf.rs`, `png.rs` `*_with`; page `web/mzdraw.js` `valuesText`, `web/index.html` `#d2-values`, `#d2-values-sel`, `#d2-style-display`.)

**Where the numbers come from.** The core has no mass table and must not get one (`mzlab-mol` of the pin `ccd4680` has only a private three-decimal average-weight list and no monoisotopic table). Counting (formula, composition, charge, the atoms an abbreviation stands for, the selection with its cut bonds) is done on the record in `values.rs`. Every MASS comes from the engine `sel` of mzLab (`mzlab_feat::chem::sel::sel_json`, `selection/1`, the same function as `mzlab-cli mol sel`) at the pinned commit `ccd4680fc35b4ca6029cfead3a8e97f82042fdf7`: `label.mass` is the monoisotopic mass, `average` the average weight (the conventional weights of `sel`, three decimals; RDKit's IUPAC weights differ by a few thousandths on halogens), `label.mz` the m/z of a charged structure. The only sum made in this repository is `mass + shift` for an adduct, the shift being `mzlab_feat::chem::sdf::adduct_shift` (the nuclide table of the same engine). The core sees the engine only as a function pointer, `values::Measurer` (`fn(molfile, adducts) -> Result<Measured, key>`), held by `Editor::measurer`; the bridge sets it (`glue::measure`), the native `mzdraw` replay has none. Without it the formulas are there and every number is `null` (`"engine": false`), never made up. A mass of an ion is the sum of its atoms (`mass`); its m/z, `ionMz`, takes the electrons off. A charged structure has no adduct (`mz` values `null`).

**Settings** (`--set`, settings file): `display.values` = `off` (default) | `formula` | `mass` | `mz`; `display.adducts` = a comma list of `[M+H]+`, `[M+NH4]+`, `[M+Na]+`, `[M+K]+`, `[M-H]-`, `[M+Cl]-`, `[M+HCOO]-`, each once, order kept (default `[M+H]+,[M-H]-`; empty is none). A wrong word or name is `error.draw2.styleValue` and changes nothing. The settings file writes them only when not default, so an old file keeps its bytes. Example: `--set display.values=mz --set display.adducts=[M+H]+,[M+Na]+`.

**Replay field** `values`: `true` or `{"atoms":[ID,...]}`; the answer is `values/1` (not a drawing): `{"format","adducts","engine","error","structures":[{"anchor","atoms","formula","charge","composition","mass","average","ionMz","mz","error"}],"selection":{"of","atoms","formula",...}|null}`. Numbers are strings with four decimals (`mzlab_mol::fixed`, half away from zero; never the float formatter). It stands alone (`error.draw2.valuesAlone` with `out`, `report`, `dry_run`, `expect`). Through the glue door (`cargo run -p mzdraw-wasm --example replay_glue`, `.wasm` `replay_glue`) a request WITHOUT `engines` is now the plain replay with the engine attached (with `engines` the old answer shape is unchanged): `{"import":"Cn1cnc2c1c(=O)n(C)c(=O)n2C","values":true}` gives `194.0804` and `[M+H]+ 195.0877`; `{"import":"CCO","set":["display.values=mass"],"out":"svg"}` draws `C2H6O  46.0419` under the structure. The plain door (`replay`) draws the formula where a mass would stand.

**Selection.** The selected atoms (with the atoms of their abbreviations) are one structure, however many pieces; a bond to an atom outside is cut and closed with one hydrogen per order of the bond, in the formula and for the engine alike (the methyl of ethanol is `CH4`, 16.0313). The page asks for it in `state().values.selection`, recomputed when the selection changes (`Editor::refresh_selection_values`).

**Page.** "Calculated values": one line per structure (`#d2-values`) and a line for the selection (`#d2-values-sel`), in words from the catalog (`draw2.values.*`), numbers taken from the module as strings. The outline keeps its words. The style panel has the select `display.values` and a checkbox per adduct (`#d2-style-display`, outside `#d2-style-fields`, whose list another test pins). JS composes sentences only. Under the drawing: the caption lines under the lowest atom of each structure (below the annotation level when there is one); the SVG, PDF and PNG page grows to hold them.

**Edits and cost.** After a change the editor rescans (one union-find pass) and asks the engine only for structures whose anchor, formula or isotopes changed (a move keeps the numbers); a held result is used only for the structure it was found for. Caps: above 5,000 record atoms or 200 structures the answer has `error.draw2.valuesSize` and no numbers (the formulas stay). One engine call per structure; sub-records are built once from per-structure lists. Tests time `scan`, `captions`, the sub-records and a selection of 4,000 atoms against a quarter of the size (bound 9 times, with a floor); they fail on a mutation that scans all atoms per structure, in debug and in release.

**Limits of the engine, shown as keys, never as numbers.** `sel` reads the structure with the graph reader of mzLab: an element without a valence rule (a metal ion such as `Na+`, an explicit hydrogen atom) is `error.grafo.element`, more than 200 atoms `error.grafo.tooBig`; the structure has its key and no number, the others keep theirs. A structure with an R group or another label that is not an abbreviation is `error.draw2.valuesOpen` (the formula says `R`). The copy-as-SVG/PNG of a selection from the context menu and `to_svg_styled` carry no engine numbers (the formula only). Isotope patterns and fragmentation are not built (mzLab engines).

**For the next slices.** `values::scan(doc)` gives structures with ids, composition, charge, anchor and box; `Scan::selection(doc, ids)` a selection; `Scan::measure(adducts, engine)` / `Selected::measure` the numbers for a given `Measurer`; `values::four` the one formatter. tp-09 (TP table) can read `Editor::values_json()` or call these; tp-07b (mass label modes) needs the same `Measurer` handed to `steplabel::plan` (the arm in the glue is `glue::measure`; rounding there is still to be decided: `four` rounds half away from zero).

**Proof and tests.** Hand sums to four decimals (caffeine 194.0804, glucose 180.0634 with its seven adducts, methylammonium 32.0500 / 32.0495, tetramethylammonium 74.0970 / 74.0964, sulfate 95.9517 / 47.9764, the methyl of ethanol as CH4 16.0313): `crates/mzdraw-wasm/tests/values.rs`; counting, selection, caps, settings, captions, scaling: `crates/mzdraw/tests/values.rs`; golden `tests/data/log/values.json` (parity: `.wasm` == native == recorded, through the glue door; two plain cases); e2e steps by mouse, keyboard, touch and pen. Independent: `python3 tools/fidelity/values.py` hands 21 synthetic structures and 4 selections to RDKit (`CalcMolFormula`, `ExactMolWt`, `MolWt`) and compares OUR `values/1`: formula, charge, mass, ionMz, the seven adducts of every neutral structure (RDKit isotope masses and the electron mass, written in the script) and average mass (tolerance 0.01): 25 of 25 agree with RDKit 2026.09.2; three structures are listed as engine limits (Na+ twice). The data source of the masses is the same (AME), so the proof is independent of the code that sums and shifts, not of the table.

**Not built.** `Alt+C` and a dedicated dialog; values for a part of a structure by lasso with the numbers pinned on the canvas; isotope patterns; the copy of a selection with numbers; the mass labels of arrows (tp-07b) and the TP table (tp-09) which use this API; i18n of the words inside the SVG caption (`m/z`, adduct names stay as written).
