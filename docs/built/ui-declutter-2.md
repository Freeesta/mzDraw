# One general tools palette, groups with one button each, and one icon set (ui-declutter-2)

The owner found too many palettes by default (two side-by-side columns of tools, each with its handle) and ugly icons. This slice replaces the six tool palettes (`tools`, `bonds`, `rings`, `atoms`, `charges`, `objects`) with ONE general palette, and redraws the icons as one set. It supersedes the table of `docs/built/palettes-page.md` for the tools; the palettes of the top row and the side palettes are as they were.

## The general tools palette (the model is `crates/mzdraw/src/tool_groups.rs`; the page paints it)
- **Sixteen places, two columns of eight** (a column palette; a row palette lays them in two rows). A place is a tool on its own, or a group. The order is fixed: select, move here, erase, bonds, double bond, rings, cyclohexane, cyclopentane | C, N, O, more elements, charges and labels, arrows and reaction objects, plus, text.
- **A group is one button.** Its face is a member: the one used last (the memory), else the group's first (single bond, benzene, the plus charge, the arrow). A click on it selects the face and opens the list of EVERY member (13 bond kinds, 7 rings, ...); a click on a list that is open shuts it; a member picked from the list is selected, becomes the face, and shuts the list. The elements and the text have a face of their own icon: a click lists them (the typed entry of an element, a label, a line of text) and selects nothing.
- **`keep`** (the kinds drawn most) stay in view on their own as before: the double bond, cyclohexane, cyclopentane, the plus sign, C, N, O. They are listed in their group too, as a second button without a test id, and never take the face (it would repeat a button that is already there).
- **The memory is the module's.** `Editor::set_tool` tells the layout; when a face changes it returns `PALETTES`, so the page lays out again and keeps the settings. The faces are the object `heads` of `layout` in the settings file, only where they differ from the first member (`{"heads":{"bonds":"bond:hash"}}`), in the order of the groups. CLI twin: `--set layout.heads.<group>=<tool>`; a face that is not a member, is in view on its own, selects no tool (a scheme row, a pathway) or belongs to a group with a fixed face is refused with `error.draw2.layout`. `layout.reset` forgets the faces.
- **The answer of `palettes(...)`** carries `rows`, `slots` (per place: `cmd`, or `group`, `face`, `icon`, `members`, `keep`, `fields`, `owns`) and, per palette, `can_collapse` (false for the tools: the general palette is always whole; its groups fold into their own lists, so the checkbox of the Palettes menu is disabled and a double click on its handle collapses nothing).
- **Flyouts, keys, touch and pen** are the ones that were: the head of a group is a `button` with `aria-expanded`; Enter or Space on it selects the face and opens the list with the focus on the member in use; the arrow key across the palette opens the list and selects nothing; the arrows walk the list; Enter picks, Escape shuts it and the focus comes back to the button; Tab leaves. Buttons are 44 px; a tap or a pen press is a click. The list of a group of many icons (bonds, rings, elements) is a grid of 44 px cells.

## A stored layout of the earlier palettes
`Layout::read` drops the entries of `bonds`, `rings`, `atoms`, `charges` and `objects` (`palettes::LEGACY`), clears the `collapsed` of an old `tools` entry (the place the user gave the tools is kept), keeps every other entry and every unknown key, and sets `migrated`. `Editor::set_style_stored` (the browser's stored settings) then answers the note `draw2.layout.migrated`, which the page shows once in the style panel's note; what is written back has no old palette, so the note does not come again. A layout that had only old entries is the default again.

## Icons (`web/icons.js`, one module)
One set, original, inline SVG, no font and no fetch: 24 px grid, stroke `WEIGHT` = 1.75 px (one constant; the wedge and the dots are filled), round caps and joins, `currentColor`, so they follow the theme and read at 20 px in the light and the dark theme. Redrawn: the gear (eight teeth and a hub), the palettes grid (four squares), the document family (open folder, save tray, new sheet, formats sheet share one outline: a body inside a 2 px margin, corners 1.5 px), the dots of More, the arrow; the bold, hashed and hydrogen bonds keep their weights relative to the new stroke. Names and tooltips are unchanged (`aria-label`, `title`). Before and after, dark and light, at deviceScaleFactor 2: `docs/shots/ui-declutter-2-before-*.png`, `ui-declutter-2-after-*.png`, and the list open: `...-flyout.png`.

## Tests
- Rust: `tool_groups.rs` (places, membership, the face, a fixed order of the entries, refused faces); `tests/palettes.rs` (sixteen places, a tool becomes the face, the face is kept and forgotten, `--set layout.heads.*`, no collapse of the tools, the migration with its note and the second read without one); golden cases in `tests/data/log/settings.json` (the faces set one by one; an old layout) and two refused ones, replayed natively and by the `.wasm` (`tests/parity.mjs`).
- e2e (Chromium): the old palette steps are retargeted to the palettes that remain (drags, docking, the menu, the tablet), and each keeps its assertions; new steps: "tool groups" (mouse: one click selects and lists, the face follows the pick, a kept kind never takes it, reload keeps the face, the Palettes menu does not list the old palettes), "by keyboard" (Enter, arrows, Escape, focus back, the arrow across), "by touch and by pen" (44 px targets, a tap and a pen press list, a tap picks and draws).
- Velocity: the guard and `baseline.json` are untouched. The recorded paths of `tests/data/velocity/mzdraw.json` press the button of a group (`head-bonds`, `head-rings`, `head-charges`) where they pressed the first member, and use the key of a member that is not in view (`w`, `Shift+w`, `-`, `Shift+a`): one action each, as before. No path got longer. A list that is open covers the canvas at its side until the next press, so a path that starts a drag there uses the key, not the button.

## The trade-off, stated plainly
The tools that were one mouse click each and are now two (the group button, then the member) are the wedges, the minus charge, the chain tool, the lone pair and the curved arrows, and every ring and bond kind but the double bond, cyclohexane and cyclopentane. The single bond, benzene, the arrow and the plus charge stay one click while they are the face of their group (the face is the member last used). The velocity guard measures the key route for the wedges, the minus charge and the arrow (`w`, `Shift+w`, `-`, `Shift+a`), which is one action as before; it does NOT measure the mouse route for them, which is longer. Touch has no key route: on touch a wedge costs two taps. A margin is thin in `three-in-a-row`: its click at -300 comes right after `head-rings`, and an open list covers the canvas beside the palette until the next press, so that path would fail if the list were wider.

## What the module decides (rule 2)
`slots` carries `grid` per group; each palette carries `next`, `prev` and `across` (the keys along it and across it); the command `group:<name>` selects the face unless it is the tool already; `pressed_groups()` names the groups the page marks with `aria-current`. The page paints and forwards. `slots` is cached for the faces it was written with, so a drag frame does not rebuild it. The button names its face (`Bonds: Single bond`), has `aria-haspopup`, and uses `aria-current` for the group in use, not `aria-pressed` beside `aria-expanded`. Every row of a list is 44 px.

## Stored settings
A stored `layout.heads` face this version cannot show (a member removed or now in view on its own, a group with a fixed face, a value that is no text) is dropped with the note `draw2.layout.heads` and the rest of the file is taken; `--set` and an explicit settings file still refuse it. Notes add up (`set_style_stored_notes`; `note` is the first, `notes` all), so the note of the migration is never hidden by another.

## Not built
- No setting or command to choose which tools stay in view (`keep` is a constant of the module); no user-made groups.
- The dotted handle rows, the default theme, the status text and the About and Report buttons are the other chat's (ui-declutter-1).
- The pathway commands (scheme row, pathway tree and label) are listed in the arrows group but never take its face: they select no tool.
- Hover and long-press peeks of a group are not built: a click or a tap is the one route.
