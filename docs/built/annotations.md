# Annotations, built

a structure carries how sure its identification is and on what evidence (`crates/mzdraw/src/ann.rs`; the field list is `docs/mzdraw-annotations.md`). The annotation is a list of entries, a history whose last entry is current: `level` (a string, never a number: `"1"`, `"2a"`, `"2b"`, `"3"`, `"4"`, `"5"` of Schymanski 2014; any other word is kept as read), `scale`, `identifier`, `evidence` (`kind`, `ref`, `note`), `scores`, `source`, `by`, `note`; keys this version does not know are kept. It lives on one atom of the structure as the field `ann` of `mzdraw/1`, set by one operation with its exact inverse (`{"op":"atom.ann","id":1,"ann":[...]}`); `Editor::annotate` adds an entry in one undo step. Out: the document (and the pictures that carry it), `L2b` under the structure in the SVG and the PNG, four data fields of an SDF record (`MZLAB_LEVEL`, `MZLAB_IDENTIFIER`, `MZLAB_EVIDENCE`, `MZLAB_ANN`), read back from them. What is not an annotation is refused with `error.draw2.ann`. On the page: the panel "Annotation of the structures". Scales (arch-std-14): the panel offers a scale, `schymanski-2014` (default) or `charbonnet-2022` (PFAS: `1a` to `5b`), from the one table `ann::SCALES`; the level list follows the scale, the entry is written with that `scale`, and a level not of the chosen scale stays allowed and is shown "of the file" (`Editor::annotate` takes the scale; no new field of `draw_edit/1`; the level list for a scale, with "of the file", is `ann::level_list`, given to the page by `ann_levels`). The meaning of each level is an i18n text `draw2.ann.level.<scale>.<level>`, a short paraphrase (the Table 1 of Charbonnet 2022 is an image that was not read: the PFAS meanings are to be checked against it). Not built: scores and source on the page, level-aware depiction of levels 4 and 5, the caption line, annotations in KET, CDXML and CML.
