# A drawn parent → product saved as `trasf/1` (plan-12), built

`Editor::save("trasf")` / `{"out":"trasf"}` in a replay request (`mzlab-cli mol draw`) / the button `trasf/1` of the Save panel (`crates/mzdraw/src/trasf.rs`). The file is three JSON lines: the record `trasf/1`, then the parent and the product as `mol/1`, both on the canonical order the record's map is written on (so the hashes in the record are theirs). The record is written by `mzlab_mol::Transformation` (`canonical` and `validate_against`); this crate only collects its inputs: no second writer. Nothing in `mzlab-mol` was changed and no new op exists (maps are plan-11's `atom.set`).

Shape: exactly one arrow, one structure before its tail, one after its head, nothing else beside it (`error.draw2.trasfShape`; a second product, such as the alcohol of a hydrolysis, is not a place in the record: draw the acid only). Map: the same number on a parent atom and a product atom is one atom; an unmapped parent atom is lost, an unmapped product atom is added (the convention of mapped reaction SMILES). The map is complete when every number occurs once per side and on both sides, and every atom of the smaller structure is mapped; otherwise `error.draw2.trasfMap`, naming the atoms (`Editor::trasf_problem`, the message of the page, the `id` of the error of a replay). To record a substitution at one atom give the old and the new atom the same number (the element changes at the centre). `delta` is the formula difference (hydrogens included, `mzlab_mol::delta::print`); `centre` is every mapped atom whose element, charge, hydrogens or bonds differ. Only an arrow and two structures may be in the drawing: a plus sign or a text (or a structure standing on the arrow) is `error.draw2.trasfShape`, never left out silently; the text of the arrow is written as the record's `rule` and drawn again on open. A group label or R group is refused (`error.draw2.trasfLabel`: its atoms carry no map); a record `mzlab-mol` refuses (e.g. a stereo centre of the change drawn as defined, no `stereo_rule`) is `error.draw2.trasfRecord`.

Open: an import of a text with a `trasf/1` line and the `mol/1` lines it names (any order) draws parent → arrow → product with the maps 1, 2, ... on the pairs (`error.draw2.trasf` for a missing, repeated or unnamed record; `error.draw2.trasfUncertain` for a record without a product; `error.draw2.trasfFields` for a record carrying `rule_set`, `rule_version`, `stereo_rule`, `level` or `evidence`, which a drawing cannot keep: refused, not dropped). The opened drawing has its own layout, so the coordinates written again may differ; the record is the same.

Decision: the uncertain form (`delta` + `sites`, no product) is NOT written. It needs `p` for every site and a region; a drawing has none, and the study says a most likely site is never written as a certain one. A drawing without a complete map is refused.

Proof: `crates/mzdraw/tests/trasf.rs` (hydroxylation `+O`, N-demethylation `-CH2`, ester hydrolysis `-CH2`, substitution `+HO-Cl`, each read back by `mzlab_mol::Transformation::from_json` and `validate_against`; refusals; the way back); `tests/data/log/trasf.json` (golden, wasm == native, sections `trasf` and `refused`); `tests/e2e.mjs` (mouse + keyboard, touch, pen); `tools/fidelity/trasf.py` (RDKit 2026.09.2: for 7 synthetic mapped reactions of `tests/data/fidelity/trasf-set.txt`, RDKit runs the INPUT reaction (`RunReactants`) on the input reactant and its product must equal our product record's InChIKey; the elements of the mapped pairs, of the lost and of the added atoms, the reaction centre and the formula change `delta` are computed from the input and compared with what we wrote; reference `tests/data/fidelity/trasf.json`). The map and centre are compared by element, not by atom index (our indices are canonical, the input's are not), so a swap between two atoms of the same element is not caught. Golden: `refused` cases for every key (`trasfShape` with a plus, a text and a structure on the arrow, `trasfMap`, `trasfLabel`, `trasfRecord` for a stereo centre drawn as defined) and `results` cases for `report` and `dry_run`. Screenshots at deviceScaleFactor 2: `docs/shots/trasf-out-*.png`.

Not proven: the RDKit reference is not read by a Rust test (its `file_hash` cannot be recomputed without RDKit's own RXN); stereo of the centre; reactions with several products or reactants; the checks above use small synthetic molecules. An RXN whose `$MOL` title line is empty (as RDKit writes), and a molfile alone with an empty title, used to be read one line short (`error.draw2.rxn`); fixed in the follow-up (the leading newline is cut off only from the records after a `$$$$` line; golden case in `tests/data/log/trasf.json`). The file is taken for a transformation by its first line (`trasf/1`, or `mol/1` when the records come first).

Not built: the uncertain form, several products, `evidence`, `stereo_rule`, `level` (left empty), choosing the two records from separate files in the page (one file holds all three lines), SMARTS and the Open Reaction Database (out of scope).

Molfile headers (follow-up): the title line may be empty; one extra blank line before the header is dropped when the counts line stands on line 5 and not 4 (so a pasted molfile with a stray leading blank line still reads, also before an empty title); two or more extra blank lines are refused, not guessed; CRLF files and SDF records with an empty title are read (tests in `crates/mzdraw/tests/trasf.rs`). A record with more mapped pairs than 999 is refused on open (`error.draw2.trasf`) before anything is drawn.
