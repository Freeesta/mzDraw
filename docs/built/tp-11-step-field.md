# The step field on the page (tp-11), built

(`crates/mzdraw/src/editor_step.rs` (field state, scoped keys, overlay, tap on a ring, `step_json`), `tp/steps.rs` (`filter`), `values.rs` (`element_masses`, `delta_mass`), `editor_tools.rs` (the field's keys come before the others while it is open), `editor_touch.rs` (`step_pointer`), `context.rs` (item `tp.step`), `proposal.rs` (`settle_proposal` also brings the field up to date), `crates/mzdraw-wasm/src/lib.rs` (`step_field`, `step_text`, `step_pick`, `step_derive`, `step_close`), page `web/mzdraw.js` (`stepField`, `stepEvents`), `web/index.html` (`#d2-step`), `web/mzdraw.css`, `web/lang/mzdraw.js` (`draw2.step.*`, `draw2.ctx.tp.step`); tests `crates/mzdraw/tests/step_field.rs`, `crates/mzdraw-wasm/tests/values.rs`; golden cases `tests/data/log/step-field.json`; e2e: four steps of `tests/e2e.mjs`; screenshots at deviceScaleFactor 2 `docs/shots/tp11-step-field.png`, `docs/shots/tp11-step-field-touch.png`; kill test `tools/velocity/step_field.mjs`, committed result `tests/data/velocity/step-field.json`.) No `draw_edit/1` op, request field or request row is new: the field is a page-only view of `out: sites`; deriving runs `tp:derive` with `step` and `site` (tp-10), whose log replayed natively gives the page's bytes (three parity cases in `step-field.json`).

**What it is.** On a drawing with the pathway features on (`pathway.derive=on`, as Derive TP) the context menu of an atom or a selection has "Apply step..." (`tp.step`, also by the Menu key and Shift+F10). It opens a one-line field with the table of tp-10 below it, filtered as you type. The chosen row (the first, or the one tapped, or Up / Down) has its sites ringed on the canvas, the focused site doubly; there a ghost shows what the step adds (a dot and a line for a new atom) or takes away (a struck ring, a heavy struck line). The overlay is in the view only: it is not in the document and makes no operation. The live line says the step, the delta and the site ("hydroxylation, +O, at C4, site 3 of 3"; an atom is its element and id, a bond its two atoms).

**Filter** (`tp::filter(text, shift)`, Rust): empty text lists every row, in table order. A text with a sign and a digit or point is a mass shift (`+15.99`): rows whose shift, with four decimals, starts with it. A text with a sign and an element is a delta: when some row has exactly that delta only those rows are listed (`+O` gives hydroxylation and n-oxidation, not `+O3S`), else the rows whose delta starts with it (`+C6` while typing). Anything else is a part of a name, any case. The mass shift is derived only from engine values, by element-mass arithmetic: `values::element_masses` (called the first time the field opens in a session, 7 engine calls, then kept in `Editor::step_masses`) asks the `Measurer` (arch-web-17, the glue) for seven one-structure molfiles with implicit hydrogens (CH4, C2H6, H2O, NH3, H2S, HCl, HBr) and solves the monoisotopic mass of H, C, O, N, S, Cl, Br from them; the core keeps no mass table. Without an engine (native `mzdraw`) a mass text lists nothing and the rows carry no mass. Hand-checked to four decimals in `crates/mzdraw-wasm/tests/values.rs` (+O 15.9949, -CH2 -14.0157, +H2O 18.0106, -CO2 -43.9898, +H-Cl -33.9610).

**Keys, only while the field is open, decided in Rust** (`Editor::key_inner`): `[` and `]` the previous and next site (round), `Enter` derives (`tp::derive_step`, one undo step, the copy becomes the selection, the field closes), `Escape` closes, Up / Down choose the previous / next row; every other key without Ctrl is not the field's and the page lets the browser type it into the text. Outside the field bare `[` and `]` stay unbound; `Alt+[` and `Alt+]` rotate the selection with the field open or not (the Alt branch comes first). Choosing "Apply step..." while the field is open starts a fresh field; the page's input follows the text the module reports, and the field follows the drawing after any change of the history (the sites are found again, the focused site stays where it still is; it closes only when its atom is gone). `Tab` is not used. The field opens on the structure of the given atom, else the focused atom, else the first selected atom; with none it says `error.draw2.tpNone`.

**Touch and pen.** A press on a ringed site focuses it (`step_pointer`; its move and release are swallowed; the reach is the tools' reach for the kind of pointer: 22 px touch, 16 px pen), the button "Derive" (or Enter) derives. A tap on a row of the list chooses it. 44 px targets on a coarse pointer.

**Kill test (tp-11): not passed, so there is no key.** `node tools/velocity/step_field.mjs` plays, in Chromium, three hydroxylation products of methylcyclohexane (two of the parent, one of the first product) with the field: right-click the parent atom, the item Apply step..., tap the ring of the site, Enter = 4 actions each, 6 for the parent, **18 in all**; the result reaches the target (4 oxygens, 28 carbons). The derive-then-edit arm is NOT replayed by that script: its counts are those of the committed path `parent-five-tps` of `tests/data/velocity/mzdraw.json` (click the parent atom, Shift+D, the O tool, a drag, Select = 5; the last of a chain 4), **20 in all**. The cut is 10 %, under the 30 % bar, so `Shift+T` is not bound (it stays the text tool, as before) and the field is behind the context menu only. With a key of its own the count would be the same 4 per product (click, Shift+T, ring, Enter), which is why the key does not pay. The task is ours, small (one step kind, three products) and not a published benchmark; the table has no step for a methyl on carbon or for a ring opening of a hydrocarbon, so the five-product task of tp-03 cannot be done with steps alone, and a site far down the list costs one tap on its ring. `tests/data/velocity/step-field.json` is the committed output.

**Proof.** `crates/mzdraw/tests/step_field.rs` (7): the filter (delta, name, mass with a stand-in shift function), bare `[` `]` do nothing with the field closed and `Alt+[` rotates (also with it open), the open field walks the sites, Enter derives with one exact undo and the document is untouched before it, the ring and ghost marks counted in the scene, Escape and unbound keys, Shift+T is still the text tool with the features on and off, a tap on a ring and the Derive call, the follow-the-drawing rule, an atom needed. Goldens in `tests/data/log/step-field.json` (hydroxylation at the third and at the second of three sites, n-oxidation) held wasm == native by `tests/parity.mjs`; e2e (keyboard through Shift+F10, touch by long press, pen by the barrel button, a mass text and a tapped row) checks the page equals those goldens after each way, the overlay is no operation, one undo. Reference programs: none new (the steps themselves are checked against RDKit in tp-10).

**Not built.** A key for the field (see the kill test); a key for a typed `[` or `]` on every layout beyond the two cases handled (plain, and AltGr arriving as Ctrl+Alt, which the field takes for `[` `]` `{` `}` only; any other Ctrl+Alt key is not the field's); sites chosen by a click on a bond with a bond step other than by its ring at the middle; names of the steps in other languages (the filter matches the English names); the mass shift of a step in the CLI (the field is page-only; `steps/1` still has no mass); a typed delta that is not a row (no search from a mass to a formula).
