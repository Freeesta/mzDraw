# Named transformation steps at a chosen site (tp-10), built

(`crates/mzdraw/src/tp/steps.rs` (the table `STEPS`, `sites`, `patch`), `tp.rs` (`derive_step`, `derive_expecting`, `sites_args`), `commands.rs` (`step` and `site` of `tp:derive`), `replay.rs` and `editor.rs` (the outputs `sites` and `steps`), `steplabel.rs` (`name+delta`), `label.rs` (`GlcA`); tests `crates/mzdraw/tests/tp_steps.rs`, `tests/steplabel.rs`; golden cases `tests/data/log/tp-steps.json`; proof `tools/fidelity/steps.py`, reference `tests/data/fidelity/steps.json`; kill-test counter `tools/fidelity/steps_cover.py`.) No `draw_edit/1` op or field is new: a step expands to a patch of existing operations. No page change (the page field is tp-11), so there are no screenshots and no new e2e step.

**The table.** Thirteen rows `name`, `delta` (canonical spelling of `mzlab_mol::delta::print`: gains first, Hill order, so `+O3S` is sulfation's `SO3` and `+H-Cl` is dechlorination's `-Cl+H`), site `kind` (`atom` or `bond`), a site test and an edit. The comment of each row cites its source: `-CH2`, `-H2`, `+O`, `+H2O`, `+C6H8O6` are listed in Molecules 2019, 24:1203, Table 4 (study `docs/study/tp-draw.md` §1 T11); `+O3S` and `+C2H2O` are computed from the formulas, and `+H-Cl`, `+H-Br` and `-CO2` are computed. The delta of the copy is checked on every derive against the counts of the record, so a row cannot say more than its edit does.

| step | kind | site test (all atoms unlabelled, neutral, no radical) | edit |
|---|---|---|---|
| `hydroxylation` | atom | C with at least one H (record) | new O bonded to it |
| `n-demethylation` / `o-demethylation` | atom | the CH3 (3 H, one neighbour) whose neighbour is N / O | remove the CH3 |
| `n-oxidation` | atom | N with no H: three single bonds and not aromatic, or aromatic with two bonds (orders 1 + 2) | new O; N `chg +1`, O `chg -1` |
| `dehydrogenation` | bond | single C-C, not aromatic, one H at least on each end | order 2 |
| `hydration` | bond | C=C, not aromatic | order 1; new O on the end with fewer H (ties: smaller id) |
| `dechlorination` / `debromination` | atom | a terminal Cl / Br | remove it |
| `hydrolysis` | bond | single, not aromatic, between a carbonyl C (a terminal O doubly bonded) and an O or N that has another heavy neighbour (ester, amide, lactone, lactam) | cut the bond; new O on the carbonyl C |
| `acetylation` | atom | N-H or O-H | new atom labelled `Ac` |
| `sulfation` | atom | O-H | new atom labelled `SO3H` |
| `glucuronidation` | atom | N-H or O-H (the O-H of a COOH included) | new atom labelled `GlcA` |
| `decarboxylation` | atom | the carbon of a CO2H (one terminal =O, one terminal OH, a third neighbour that is not a terminal O) | remove the C and its two O |

An atom whose hydrogens are fixed by hand (`h` of the atom) has the count moved by the edit, so the formula stays right. `GlcA` is a new row of the abbreviation table of `label.rs` (the glucuronic acid residue as a glycoside, `C6H9O6`, no stereo: `C1OC(C(=O)O)C(O)C(O)C1O`), also a unit of a longer label; it expands at export like the other abbreviations.

**Limits, said plainly.** Hydrogen counts are the record's (`Document::to_mol`). An atom with a label (an abbreviation, an R group) is never a site and is an unknown neighbour (neither N nor O). A hydrogen drawn as an atom of element H is a neighbour like any other and is not looked through. Aromaticity is perceived by `mzlab-mol` from the Kekule form; a structure with fewer bonds than atoms has no ring, so the perception is not run on it. A new atom is put one median bond length from the site along the bisector of the widest angle between the site's neighbours; nothing else of the drawing is looked at, so it can land on another atom (scaffold kept, nothing is cleaned up, as in tp-03). No stereo is created. `n-oxidation` accepts an amine of any kind (an amide N too) and an aromatic N with two bonds. `hydrolysis` needs the O or N to have another heavy neighbour, so a primary amide (`C(=O)NH2`) and an acid are not sites. `hydrolysis` of a ring leaves one structure; of a chain, two (the TP holds both).

**Sites.** `tp::sites(doc, of, step)` lists the ids (atoms or bonds as the kind says) where the test holds in the structure that holds the atom `of`, ascending: a list to choose from, never a ranking. A structure above 5,000 atoms is `error.draw2.tpSize` (the cap of `tp:derive`), before any work.

**Derive by step.** `{"command":"tp:derive","args":{"of":1,"step":"o-demethylation","site":14}}`; `step` and `site` take the place of `patch` (both together, or either alone, is `error.draw2.request`; `at` still places the copy). A site where the test fails, or that is no atom / bond of the structure as the kind says, is `error.draw2.tpSite` (a verdict: exit 1, `failed.stage` `command`, nothing changes). An unknown step is `error.draw2.tpStep` (an invalid request, exit 2). A copy whose delta is not the step's is `error.draw2.tpDelta`, nothing written: `tp::derive_expecting` runs it for any patch (the formula counts of the record: the copy minus the parent, every fragment of the copy). The log holds plain operations, so a page or a CLI replay gives the same bytes.

**Outputs.** `{"out":"sites","args":{"of":1,"step":"hydroxylation"}}` gives `{"delta":"+O","format":"sites/1","ids":[...],"kind":"atom","step":"hydroxylation"}`; `{"out":"steps"}` gives `steps/1`: the table (`name`, `delta`, `kind`). The mass shift is not in it: arch-web-17 is not on `main`. `args` without a command is accepted only with `out` `sites`. Keys (texts in `web/lang/mzdraw.js`): `error.draw2.tpSite`, `error.draw2.tpStep`, `error.draw2.tpDelta`.

**`pathway:label` `name+delta` (left by tp-07).** The mode writes the name of the one step whose delta is the label, then the delta: `dehydrogenation -H2`. A delta that two steps share (`+O`: hydroxylation and n-oxidation, `-CH2`, `+H2O`) or that no step has is written alone (no guess). A step name is one of the tokens the command owns, so a label that is only names and deltas is rewritten, and `hydroxylation (CYP)` stays the person's. `error.draw2.labelSteps` is gone (key, text, test); `mass` and `delta+mass` are still refused (`error.draw2.labelMass`).

**Request rows** (`schema::REQUEST`, D13): the `tp:derive` row (args `step`, `site`; the keys `tpSite`, `tpStep`, `tpDelta`), the rows `out` `steps` and `out` `sites` (with the request field `args`), and the `pathway:label` / `pathway.label` rows. `docs/mzdraw.md` and `tests/data/log/edit-schema.json` moved by that.

**Cost.** One derive by step costs the structure (one pass for the structure, the record, the neighbour tables, the flags) plus the cost of `derive` (tp-03). Tests (`tp_steps.rs`) isolate this slice's code from the import and from `tp:derive` of tp-03, whose superlinear cost is not this slice's: the step derive is timed against the plain derive of the same edit on the same prebuilt drawing (2,000 and 8,000 structures, bound 1.5 times plus a floor), and `sites` of five steps is timed alone on a prebuilt chain of 600 and of 2,400 atoms (bound 9 times for a 4-fold size, with a floor). Both fail on a quadratic mutation of the code of the slice (a lookup per bond in the structure, a record rebuilt per atom) in debug and in release (the structure without a ring skips the aromatic perception, which is not linear on a long chain). The perception of aromatic rings in `mzlab-mol` is itself above linear on a long chain of rings (400 rings: 0.5 s, 800: 1.9 s in release, the same as the import alone), bounded by the 5,000-atom cap.

**Proof.** `tests/tp_steps.rs` (13): the table (names, canonical deltas, kinds), for every row a parent that has the site (the TP's formula, as the digest says it, is the parent plus the delta) and one that lacks it (`sites` empty, the derive is a verdict `tpSite`), `sites` ascending and equal to a brute force over every atom and every bond id of four parents (each id given to the derive: accepted or `tpSite`), the rules that tell the cases apart (a quaternary C, a CH3 on C, a pyrrole N, a charged N, an aromatic ring bond, a ketone, an acid, a carboxylate), charges of the N-oxide, the delta check on a wrong patch, undo and redo of every step (identical document) and the log replayed without the import, bad shapes (exit 2), the size cap, scaling. Goldens wasm == native in `tp-steps.json`: n-demethylation, hydrolysis, glucuronidation, a TP of a TP, `steps`, `sites` of an atom and of a bond step, the refusal, and derive then `name+delta`. `pathway-label.json`: one case moved on purpose, "pathway.label=name+delta" was the refusal `error.draw2.labelSteps` and is now the label (4 lines: its name and its recorded output). **Independent proof:** `python3 tools/fidelity/steps.py` (RDKit 2026.09.2, 2026-11-21) runs ONE reaction SMARTS per row (in the tool only, no SMARTS in the editor) on 12 synthetic parents at every atom or bond (the site is marked by an isotope) and compares with OUR output read as a molfile: **site lists 156/156, products 110/110** (canonical SMILES without stereo and maps). A wrong edit makes it fail (tried: reversing the end of the hydration, and dropping the H condition of the hydroxylation: 147/156 site lists). What it does not show: that a site is the one a chemist would choose (nothing is ranked), coordinates, the new atom's place, stereo (the parents have none), a tie of hydration between two different ends, hydrolysis of a ring (the SMARTS gives one product that both templates claim); the SMARTS and the table were written by the same author. The tool helps RDKit give the pyrrole-type N its hydrogen back (RDKit does not).

**Kill test (tp-10).** The 12-row list of tp-08 is not on `main` (tp-08 is open), so the test could not be run on it. `python3 tools/fidelity/steps_cover.py --replay target/debug/examples/replay LIST.tsv` counts, for a `parent<TAB>product` list, the products that one step at one site makes (as the whole TP or as one fragment of it). On the stand-in list written for the tool (`tests/data/fidelity/steps-cover.tsv`, 12 pairs written by the author of the table, so biased towards the table) it counts 11 of 12; that is a smoke value, NOT the kill test. To run when tp-08 is merged; below half, tp-11 should not run.

**Not built.** The page field and the overlay (tp-11). The mass shift in `steps/1` and in `name+delta` (arch-web-17, tp-07b). Any ranking, any reaction rule or SMARTS in the editor, a typed delta, a search from a mass to a formula. A step on a labelled atom or on an explicit H atom. Stereo of the new atoms. A property test over random parents (the cases are written by hand and the brute-force check covers four parents).
