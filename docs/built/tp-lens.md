# The diff lens (tp-04), built

(`crates/mzdraw/src/tp.rs` (`lens`, `lenses`, `Lens`, `Lenses`), `scene.rs` (`lens_paint`, `Painter::ghosts`, colour `MUTED`), `settings.rs` (`LensMode`, `display.lens`), `conform.rs` + `pathway.rs` (`std.tp.unmapped`), `editor_pathway.rs` (`cycle_lens`), `editor_tools.rs` (Shift+L), `context.rs` (item `canvas.lens`), `crates/mzdraw-wasm/src/lib.rs` (`state().lens`, `state().outline[].lens`), `web/` (colour `--d2-muted`, the Style choice, the outline line); tests `crates/mzdraw/tests/lens.rs` and `scene.rs`; golden cases `tests/data/log/tp-lens.json`; e2e: three steps of `tests/e2e.mjs`; screenshots at deviceScaleFactor 2 `docs/shots/tp04-{off,tint,ghost}.png`; independent check `tools/fidelity/lens.py`, reference `tests/data/fidelity/lens.json`.) No `draw_edit/1` op or field is new: the lens is display and a read-only output.

- **What is compared.** For an anchored arrow from structure P to structure T (the connected pieces that hold its two anchor atoms; a free end, or both ends in one piece, has no lens), atoms are matched by map number. `added`: atoms of T whose map P lacks. `added_bonds`: bonds of T with an added end, **and bonds between two atoms both structures have that P did not bond** (a ring closure; the prompt did not list it, and a formed bond that nothing shows would be a hole in a diff). `changed`: atoms in both whose element, label, charge, isotope, radical or hydrogen count differ (hydrogens as `Document::hydrogens` gives them, so a carbon that gains a substituent is changed). `changed_bonds`: bonds in both (same two maps) whose order differs (a wedge change is not a change here). `cleaved`: bonds of P between two maps both in T that T does not bond, as the two T atom ids (smaller first). `lost`: maps of P that T lacks (listed, never drawn). `delta` and the outline's "from": **the counts of `pathway:label`** (`steplabel::Formulas` and `steplabel::pair_delta`, one implementation: the record's Hill counts, abbreviations expanded, difference by `mzlab_mol::delta::print`); "from" is the Hill formula of P (`outline::hill`). Where `pair_delta` has no honest text (a free-text label, or equal counts with a different net charge) `delta` is `null` and the page has no words for that product, as `pathway:label` leaves such an arrow alone.
- **`"out":"lens"`** gives `lens/1`: `{"format":"lens/1","arrows":[{"arrow","added","added_bonds","changed","changed_bonds","cleaved","lost","delta"}],"skipped":[{"arrow","why"}]}`, keys in that fixed order, ids ascending, arrows by id, deterministic; the table of the keys is the `out` row `lens` of the `request` table (`docs/mzdraw.md`). **`added_bonds` and `changed_bonds` are two keys beyond the prompt's list**: atom ids and bond ids are separate namespaces. **`skipped`**: nothing is dropped in silence; an anchored arrow between two structures that has no entry in `arrows` is here with `why` `no maps` (neither structure has a map), `unmapped` (partly mapped; also the finding) or `over cap`. One row each in the `request` table (`out` `lens`, `set` `display.lens`, `std` `std.tp.unmapped`).
- **Unmapped pairs.** A pair where some atom has a map and another has none, or two atoms of one structure share a map, has no entry in `lens/1` and is the finding `std.tp.unmapped` (info; `objects`: the arrow, `atoms`: the atoms of both structures without a map or sharing one): never a partial lens. **A pair with no map at all has no lens and no `std/1` finding** (decided by the coordinator: `std` stays quiet for plain pathways) but is listed in `skipped` (`no maps`) when `out:lens` is asked, and the page says so in a note when the lens is on.
- **`display.lens`** `off` (default) | `tint` | `tint+ghost`: key `lens` of the settings file (written only when not off), `--set display.lens=...`, a choice in the Style panel (words from the schema's `display.lens`), Shift+L cycles (a display setting: no document change, no undo step, said in the live line `draw2.say.lens`), and the canvas menu item `canvas.lens` ("Diff lens", checked when on), offered only where an arrow is anchored (so long-press by touch and the barrel button by pen reach it). `tint`: added and changed atoms (their label text) and added and changed bonds of T in the accent token (colour 11, `--mzd-accent`; `#2d5d8c` in a file). `tint+ghost`: also each cleaved bond as a thin dashed line (short lines of the display list) between the two T atoms, muted token (colour 12, `--mzd-ink-muted`; `#5c5850` in a file), painted before the bonds. The lens is in no molfile, SMILES, KET, CDXML, CML, RXN or SDF (tested byte for byte, lens on and off); in an SVG, PDF or PNG only when the setting is on at export.
- **Outline.** Each product has a line in "What is drawn": "From C6H11NO: +O." with ", 1 bond cleaved" when there is one, or "no change of formula"; the Rust state carries `{from, delta, cleaved}` and the page only fills `draw2.outline.lens`. Shown while `display.lens` is not off (nothing is computed for the page while it is off); the table of products is by anchor, one lookup per outline piece (`tp::LensState`).
- **Caps and notes.** A structure above 5000 atoms, or more than 2,000,000 atoms compared over all arrows: the arrow is `skipped` with `over cap` (the rest after the budget is spent too). A drawing above 6000 atoms is `error.draw2.lensSize` for `out:lens` before any work (golden: a 6001-atom SMILES). With the lens on, the page shows a keyed note instead of nothing (`draw2.notice.lensSkipped`, `draw2.notice.lensSize`, `state().lensNote`, the line `#d2-lens-note`): the lens never fails silently. One table per drawing, one pass per arrow; `std` and the display each make their own pass (the finding is independent of the setting).
- **Cost.** `tests/lens.rs` (`the_cost_of_the_lens_grows_linearly_with_the_drawing`, 200 and 800 pairs repeated, threshold 8x against a linear 4x and a quadratic 16x; `the_state_of_the_lens_for_the_page_finds_a_product_by_its_anchor_in_one_lookup`, 16000 and 64000 products) and `scene::tests::the_lens_painting_is_linear_in_the_drawing` fail when the lens tables are rebuilt per arrow, when the table of products is scanned instead of looked up, or when each ghost scans the atoms (each tried by hand, in release). Found, not fixed here (not this slice's): `svg` of a drawing is quadratic in its number of structures (the text alternative, `outline::pieces`: a pass over all atoms per structure; 500 to 1000 TPs: 0.7 s to 2.7 s in debug), and the painter's bond pass looks atoms up by a scan.
- **Proof.** `tests/lens.rs` (18): empty lens for a copy, the fixed key order, hydroxylation, k added atoms (1..6), ring opening cleaves exactly the removed bond, changed order / lost / formed bond, the formula of P plus the delta is the formula of T for 50 seeded random patches (`delta::add`; 25 or more valid, the others refused by valence), no file format changes with the lens, the SVG has the accent only with the lens on and the muted ink only with the ghost, the setting round trip and its refusals, Shift+L, partly mapped and unmapped pairs, caps and the skipped reasons, the shared counts with `pathway:label`, the i18n text of each key, the canvas menu item. Goldens wasm == native in `tp-lens.json` (the three TPs of tp-03 with the lens off, tint and tint+ghost, a partly mapped pair, `out:lens` envelopes including `skipped`, a refused word, `error.draw2.lensSize`). e2e: Shift+L (key, then the picture equals the golden of each mode, document and undo untouched), the Style panel, the outline words and the skipped note, touch (long press, tap) and pen (barrel button, then the item). `tools/fidelity/lens.py` (RDKit 2026.09.2, 2026-11-21, 10/10): RDKit reads our RXN with maps and lists added, changed, cleaved, lost and the element-count change from its own atoms and bonds; our `lens/1`, translated to maps, equals it on ten synthetic pairs (a copy, hydroxylation, demethylation, hydrolysis, a bond order, a ring closure, an element, an isotope, an N-oxide charge, a grown chain); a wrong expectation makes it fail (tried). It does NOT show that the maps are right (our own numbering), nor the painting.

Not built: any comparison without maps; a colour the user chooses; a lens on a pair whose structures hold more than 5000 atoms; a page toggle on the toolbar (key, Style panel and canvas menu only). The kill test (the owner, or two mass spectrometrists, looking at the three goldens with the lens off and on) has not been run: `docs/shots/tp04-*.png` are what to show. Limits seen in the screenshots: the accent blue is close to the element blue of N, so a tinted nitrogen label differs little from an untouched one (the tinted bonds carry it); a ghost on a short bond is a few dashes. If they would not keep the tint, the setting stays `off` with no page toggle and `out:lens` and the outline line stay.
