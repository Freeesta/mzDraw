# Slice 2a, built

wedge marks on single bonds (`s` of a bond: solid, hashed, wavy; narrow end = atom `a`; tools `bond:up` / `bond:down` / `bond:wavy`, keys `W`, `Shift+W`, `Y`; a second click turns the wedge around). The marks are the drawing: the molfile carries them as drawn and the stereo of the record is derived by the molfile rules of `mzlab-mol`; a SMILES or molfile with stereo arrives with the wedges `mzlab-mol` writes for its record on our coordinates. Operations `rotate` and `flip` (commands on the selection or on everything; a flip keeps the marks, so it gives the other enantiomer). Clipboard: copy, cut, paste, duplicate (`Ctrl+C/X/V/D` and buttons); the system clipboard carries a molfile as plain text and the document under `application/x-mzdraw+json`. The log language gained `bond.add.s`, `bond.set` with optional `o`, `s`, `rev`, and the ops `rotate` and `flip`.
