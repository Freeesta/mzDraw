# Palettes on the page, built (PR 2 of the UI work; screenshots at deviceScaleFactor 2 in `docs/shots/palettes-*.png`)

Every group of actions is a palette (`web/palettes.js`): tools, bonds, rings, atoms, charges and labels, reaction and text, file, history, edit, view, and the six side palettes (add a structure, style, data, annotation, check, keyboard help). The page builds them, measures them, sends the pointer (mouse, touch, pen) and key events of their handles to `palette(...)` and paints what `palettes(...)` answers: where each one is, its edge, row or column, collapsed or hidden. A collapsed palette shows its head, which shows the kind last used; the rest is in a flyout reached by click or by the arrow key across the palette. Handles are 24 px for a mouse and 44 px for touch and pen; a double click collapses a palette. The Palettes menu of the File palette shows, hides, collapses and turns each palette, resets the layout and hides them all (`\` on the canvas does the same; a Palettes button brings them back). The layout is kept with the settings file in the browser (OPFS). Save writes `export.default` (SVG unless set); Save as lists the 12 formats. The canvas takes the free area. The ids and storage names the scripts write are in `web/names.js`, the icons (original) in `web/icons.js`. The tools palette holds Select, Move here and Erase; the drawing checks `std/1` list is a section of the Check palette; a side palette is capped at a share of the page height (less on a touch screen, whose handles and buttons are larger) so the right column does not wrap into the canvas, and what does not fit scrolls inside it. The `\` key returns the flag `PALETTES` (128), which the page consumes: it lays the palettes out again and keeps the settings. Covered by two e2e steps (desktop: four edges, floating, snapped, keyboard, menu, `\`, reset, built keys only in tooltips; tablet: 44 px handles, drags by finger and pen, menu by tapping).

Review round: what is decided is in Rust, the page paints it. `palettes(...)` answers the palette being dragged (`held`), the edge it docks on if let go now (`hint_edge`, the one `release` uses) and, per palette, whether it can turn (`can_turn`: only a floating one). The drag threshold is the module's (`DRAG_START`, 4 px): the page always forwards press, move, release and cancel, and a click with a little jitter moves nothing and stores nothing. The handle is named by `aria-label` and says its keys in `aria-description` (arrows, Shift, Enter, Escape, double click, the backslash key). The backslash key also works with the focus on a palette button or handle (not in a text field). The menu boxes are drawn with the page's colours, 44 px on a touch screen; the grip dots have their own token (`--grip`); hover styles apply only where there is a hover. A side palette that cannot show all of its content scrolls and shows a shadow at the edge with more behind it. The e2e checks a reload restores the layout, the jitter click, the handle's description and the key from a handle; the desktop shots are taken with a mouse and keyboard only (`docs/shots/palettes-*.png`, tablet: `palettes-tablet-side.png` without the menu and `palettes-tablet-menu.png` with it).

Velocity: the kinds drawn most stay in view in a collapsed palette (`keep` in `SPEC` of `web/palettes.js`): single and double bond, the two wedges, benzene, cyclohexane, cyclopentane, the two charges, the arrow and the plus; the head of a palette holds the rest in its flyout. The left side is two columns at 720 px. `tests/velocity.mjs` counts a tool that is not in view as the presses needed to open its flyout (the head of a collapsed palette first runs the kind last used, then opens the flyout), so a hidden tool costs actions and the guard (`--check`) sees it; the baseline is unchanged. The head of a collapsed tool palette shows and runs the kind used last, kept in view or in the flyout; on a fresh page it is the first kept kind (the single bond, benzene), never the first kind of the flyout. The e2e step "collapsed palettes" runs on a fresh page and fails with the head seeded from the flyout; the kept kinds are checked visible, by keyboard (Enter), by touch (44 px, a finger runs them) and with no overlap at 720 px. A side palette's disclosure is synced with the module's `collapsed` both ways; while the `toggle` of a click on its title is on its way a layout does not put the old state back (it had closed a palette just opened, now and then).
