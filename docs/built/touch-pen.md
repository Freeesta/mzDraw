# Touch and pen, built

(`crates/mzdraw/src/editor_touch.rs`; the page forwards every pointer with its id and time stamp and decides nothing): one finger uses the tool; two fingers pan and zoom the view around the point between them and never draw (the press of the first finger is dropped when the second lands, and a finger left over does not start to draw); a finger that lands within half a second of a pen event, or while the pen is down, is a palm and is ignored until it lifts, as is a third finger; a finger held still for half a second on an atom, a bond or an object selects it whatever the tool (the tool is kept); the eraser end of a pen (button 5) erases for one stroke and the tool comes back; a pointer the system takes away (`pointercancel`) commits nothing — before, it was treated as a release and could draw. The middle of a bond (30 % to 70 % of its length) now hits the bond even within reach of an atom: at the default zoom a finger reaches 22 px and half a bond is 20 px, so a bond could not be touched at all. Not built: pen pressure and tilt, a setting for "finger pans, pen draws", double-tap, measurements on a real tablet (the tests drive Chromium's touch emulation).
