# One-line scheme layout, built

(plan-13; `crates/mzdraw/src/scheme.rs`, the command `scheme:row` in `commands.rs`, `Editor::scheme_row`; tests `crates/mzdraw/tests/scheme.rs`, golden cases `tests/data/log/scheme-row.json`; screenshots `docs/shots/plan-13-scheme-row*.png`.) `scheme::row(doc, style, opts)` gives `move` operations (and one `obj.set` of the head of each free arrow) that put the structures (connected pieces), arrows, plus signs and texts of a drawing, or of the selection, on one baseline: equal gaps of `gap` bond lengths (default 1.0, above 0), each structure's box (`route::structure_box`, labels included) centred on the line, each plus sign in a slot of 0.5 of its own, each arrow as long as its notes ask (widest note or own text plus 0.5 at each end, at least 2.0), and `wrap` (bond lengths, default 0: never) breaking the row after an arrow that ends past that width, the next line under the first. The main direction is the way the first arrow points (left to right when there is none); the order is by centre along it. Roles are those of `rxn::to_rxn`: a structure whose centroid lies between tail and head of an arrow is an agent; with the texts within 3 bond lengths of the arrow it is a note, stacked above or below the line (by where it stands now) and centred on the arrow. The baseline is the first arrow's line. One undo step; the log carries `move` and `obj.set`, no new operation; a second run gives no operations.

Anchors (E27): an arrow with both `from` and `to` gets no end set; its structures move and `route::route` gives its ends. An arrow with only one anchor is laid out as a free arrow. The gap argument `gap` is not tp-05's `pathway.col_gap`.

CLI: `{"command":"scheme:row","args":{"gap":1,"wrap":0,"atoms":[],"objects":[]}}` (or in `commands`); `atoms`/`objects` are the selection, absent means everything; a bad `gap`, `wrap` or an unknown key is `error.draw2.request`. Page: the button `scheme-row` of the objects palette (`command("scheme-row")`), selection or everything. Coordinates are kept to four decimals, so a gap is exact to about 3e-4, not 1e-9.

Not built: orienting cores (plan-14); catalytic cycles; several rows with branching arrows; a main direction other than horizontal (arrows are made level); the `wrap` of a one-arrow scheme changes the RXN roles of what follows the break (use it for multi-step schemes); a gap argument on the button (the page uses the default); the velocity count of "two-component reaction" before and after (not measured here).
