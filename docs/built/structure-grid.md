# A grid of structures with their legends, built (rdkit-compare 8)

Found by the RDKit comparison (`docs/study/rdkit-compare.md` matrix #48; RDKit `MolsToGridImage(molsPerRow, legends)`): a table of compounds or of transformation products is a grid of structures, each with its name under it. mzDraw had the names (`display.titles`, rdkit-compare 5) and a one-line layout (`scheme:row`), not a grid.

**Command.** `grid` (`crates/mzdraw/src/grid.rs`), on the CLI route `{"command":"grid","args":{"cols":3,"gap":1,"order":"title","atoms":[]}}` (or in `commands`, with `report`, `dry_run` and `expect` as for the other commands). Every argument is optional: `cols` (1 to 100000; default the smallest number whose square holds the structures), `gap` (bond lengths between cells, above 0, default 1), `order` (`drawing`: the order of the structures in the document, the default; `reading`: rows from the top, left to right, as they stand; `title`: by the legend, numbers compared as numbers so `TP2` comes before `TP10`, the structures without a title last in the order of the drawing), `atoms` (the selection: the structures that hold these atoms; default every structure). A bad argument is `error.draw2.request`, an atom the drawing does not have `error.draw2.noAtom`; nothing changes.

**Layout.** Every cell has the width of the widest structure or caption of the set; a row is as tall as its tallest structure above its lowest atom and its deepest caption below it; `gap` lies between columns and between rows. In a row the lowest atoms stand on one line, so the legends and the values (`display.values`, `atom.cap`) of a row stand on one line, each centred under its structure. The room kept for a caption is the room the pictures give it (`svg::laid_out_with`: the same depth and width), with the values the editor holds, so nothing of one cell reaches another. The grid starts at the highest lowest atom and the leftmost middle of the structures as they stand: a second `grid` with the same arguments, or with `order: reading`, gives no operation. Arrows, plus signs and texts are not laid out; an arrow anchored at its structures follows them (`Editor::following`).

**Operations.** A list of `move` operations, one per structure that moves, run as one undo step: no new operation, the log carries them, a native replay of the log gives the page's bytes.

**Page.** The button Structure grid (`structure-grid`, group of the objects of the palette, icon in `web/icons.js`) runs `grid` with its defaults on the selection or on everything (`Editor::grid`); a second press says `draw2.say.gridDone` and adds no step. Texts in `web/lang/mzdraw.js`. Screenshots at deviceScaleFactor 2, written by the e2e step: `docs/shots/rdkit-compare-8-grid-canvas.png` (after the keyboard) and `-touch.png` (after a finger); `docs/shots/rdkit-compare-8-grid-svg.png` is the SVG the CLI writes for the golden case "five named structures in a grid by title", put in a page with `page.setContent` and taken with `locator("svg").screenshot()` in Chromium at deviceScaleFactor 2.

**The legends, from the review of rdkit-compare 5.**
- A title is one line (`values::legend`): each run of control characters is one blank and the ends are trimmed, in the caption (`captions_titled`) and in the title line of the files (`Document::title`). The routes of the page and the CLI already refuse such a title (`atom.title` and the document reader: `error.draw2.title`); a document held in memory by a program can carry one, and the SVG, the PDF, the CDXML and the molfile the PDF carries then draw the same single line the caption height counts.
- Odd titles (`<`, `&`, `"`, `'`, letters outside ASCII, an empty title, one of blanks) are escaped in the SVG and the CDXML, written in WinAnsi with `?` for what it has not in the PDF, and an empty or blank title draws no line.
- A long title (80 characters, the longest a document holds) widens the page, centred on its structure, with nothing cut.
- The ids (`display.ids`) keep off the caption lines: an id takes the free place nearest its choice that covers no caption line (the caption boxes are indexed by cell, so the work stays linear), in the canvas and in the pictures, with or without the diff lens.

Tests: `crates/mzdraw/src/grid.rs` (the natural order, the arguments); `crates/mzdraw/tests/grid.rs` (rows and columns, legends of a row on one line, no cell reaching another for five argument sets; the orders and a second grid that moves nothing; the selection and the refused requests; the button, one undo step, the CLI and the log replaying to the page's bytes, a dry run; an anchored arrow that follows; the work linear in the structures); `crates/mzdraw/tests/display_titles.rs` (odd titles in the SVG, the PDF and the CDXML; a title with line breaks; a long title and the page; the ids clear of the captions with the lens, in the canvas and the SVG); golden `tests/data/log/grid.json` (eight cases, five refused requests), wasm == native in `tests/parity.mjs`; e2e step "structure grid (rdkit-compare 8)" (keyboard, touch, pen; the page equals the golden, one undo step, the second press).

Not built: a fixed cell size (RDKit `subImgSize`: a cell here fits its content); highlights given per cell (`highlightAtomLists`; the colour of atoms and bonds is its own operation); a grid of the records of an SDF in one step on the page (import, then the button; on the CLI the two in one request).
