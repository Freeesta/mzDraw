# Reaction SMILES and dative SMILES read, built (rdkit-compare 7)

Found by the RDKit comparison (`docs/study/rdkit-compare.md` F4, F5, matrix #6 and #7; RDKit `ReactionFromSmarts(useSmiles=True)` and its `->` dative bonds): `CC(=O)O.OCC>>CC(=O)OCC` and `[NH3]->[Pd](Cl)(Cl)<-[NH3]` were `error.draw2.smiles`, though the same reaction as an RXN file was drawn and the document has dative bonds.

**Where.** `crates/mzdraw/src/rsmiles.rs`, a pass before the SMILES reader of `mzlab-mol`, which knows neither: no pin move (rule 9), no new dependency. `io::import` calls it for each line of a SMILES file (`smiles_molecule`).

**Reaction SMILES.** `reactants>agents>products`, each side a list of molecules joined by `.` (Daylight; a side may be empty, not both). `rsmiles::reaction` cuts the line at its two `>` (outside brackets, and not the `>` of a dative `->`); each molecule is read as any SMILES (its dative bonds, R groups and atom maps), and `rxn::reaction_of` lays them out as a reaction file is laid out (`rxn::from_rxn`, the same function): the reactants with plus signs between them, the arrow as long as the agents need, the agents above it, the products. So the roles of `rxn::to_rxn` (before the tail, past the head, alongside) give the reactants, the products and the agents back, and `scheme:row` lays it out again by the same rule. The name after the line is the title of its first structure. A line with one `>` or more than two, or with a molecule the reader refuses, is a broken line (`draw2.import.smilesLine` with its number; the other lines are drawn).

**Dative bonds.** `->` runs from the donor before it to the acceptor after it, `<-` from the atom after it to the one before it (as RDKit writes them). `rsmiles::datives` writes each as a single bond for the reader and gives its ends as indices of the atoms in the order of the text (the order of the document's atoms after the import); the import makes that bond of the kind `dative` (`bonddisp::KINDS`) from the donor (`a`) to the acceptor (`b`). A dative bond takes no part in the valence of its ends, so an organic-subset donor keeps its hydrogens: `N->[Cu+2]<-N` is two ammonia on copper (written `[NH3][Cu+2][NH3]`). A dative bond that is not between two atoms written next to each other (to a ring closure, before a branch, across a dot) is a broken line.

**The live check.** The structure check of the page (`check/1` of mzLab) reads the record, where a dative bond is a single bond, so it flags the donor's valence (the circled N of `docs/shots/rdkit-compare-7-dative-smiles.png`), and the masses are not given for such a structure (`error.draw2.valuesLink`): as for a dative bond drawn by hand (`built/arch-web-20-bond-kinds.md`), not new here.

**Not written.** The SMILES writer writes a reaction as its molecules joined by `.` and a dative bond as a single bond; both are `loss` items already (`object.arrow`, `bond.dative` in `docs/mzdraw-formats.md`): the RXN, V3000, KET and CDXML writers keep them.

**CLI.** `{"import": "CC(=O)O.OCC>[H+]>CC(=O)OCC.O", "out": "rxn"}` (or any `out`); the page reads the same text in Add a structure, paste and open (one route).

Tests: `crates/mzdraw/src/rsmiles.rs` (the cut of a reaction and the ends of dative bonds, refusals); `crates/mzdraw/tests/reaction_smiles.rs` (roles of a reaction with an agent, without agents, a side empty, atom maps kept, the title; dative bonds from the donor, the hydrogens of an organic-subset donor, `<-`, a dative bond in a reaction; refused lines and the broken line of a file; the CLI and the page give the same bytes, `scheme:row` keeps the roles); golden `tests/data/log/reaction-smiles.json` (five cases, five refused lines), wasm == native in `tests/parity.mjs`; e2e step "reaction and dative SMILES (rdkit-compare 7)" (keyboard, touch, pen; a refused line draws nothing). `tools/rdkit_compare/reference.json` regenerated with RDKit 2026.09.2 (`--no-time`): the rows `reaction` and `dative` are read now (it also records what rdkit-compare 4, 5 and 8 changed in the probes).
