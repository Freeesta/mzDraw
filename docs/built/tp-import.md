# Import a TP list and number the TPs (tp-08), built

(`crates/mzdraw/src/tp_list.rs`, `commands.rs` (`tp:import`, `tp:number`), `editor.rs` (`import_list`, `place_ops`, `failed_rows`), `scene.rs` (the name under the structure), `settings.rs` (`pathway.number`), `replay.rs` (`failed.rows`); tests `crates/mzdraw/tests/tp_list.rs`; golden cases `tests/data/log/tp-import.json`; the synthetic 12-row list `tests/data/tp/tp12.csv`; independent check `tools/fidelity/tpimport.py`, reference `tests/data/fidelity/tpimport.json`.) No `draw_edit/1` op or field is new: the import and the numbering write plain operations (`atom.add`, `bond.add`, `atom.data`, `atom.ann`, `obj.add` with `from`/`to`, `rotate`, `flip`, `move`, `obj.set`), so a log of them replays to the same bytes natively and in the `.wasm`.

## The list (stable; tp-09 reads it)

Twelve rows of the list file, columns in this order, a header on line 1: `smiles,name,parent,mz,rt,adduct,level` and the optional `area` (eight columns in all).

| column | meaning | check |
|---|---|---|
| `smiles` | the structure; one connected piece, 1 to 500 atoms and at most 5,000 characters (read from the text before the structure is built), no white space | `tpRowSmiles`, `tpRowSize` |
| `name` | the shown name; empty: numbered by the import | unique (`tpRowDup`); no `;`, no line break (`tpRowName`) |
| `parent` | the `name` of another row; `;` between several parents (a convergent TP); empty: a root | `tpRowParent`, `tpRowCycle` |
| `mz` | observed m/z, a number above 0 | `tpRowNumber` |
| `rt` | minutes, a number from 0 | `tpRowNumber` |
| `adduct` | a word of the form `[M<terms>]<n><+\|->` (`[M+H]+`, `[M+H-NH3]+`, `[M+2H]2+`, `[M-H]-`, `[M]+`): at most 32 characters of letters, digits and signs, each sign followed by a letter or digit, a charge of up to two digits and a sign after the bracket. **Syntax only** (`tp_list::adduct_ok`): the typed word is kept as data; whether it is a known adduct and whether the m/z fits the structure are not judged here (the table of adducts is chemistry; its validation comes through the glue with arch-web-17) | `tpRowAdduct` |
| `level` | a confidence level word (`ann::word`: letters, digits, `.`, `-`, `_`, at most 16) | `tpRowLevel` |
| `area` | a number from 0; kept, never drawn | `tpRowNumber` |

CSV is RFC 4180: `"` quotes a field, `""` is a quote, a quoted field may hold a line break, CRLF and a BOM are read. The header may name any of the eight columns once each in any order and must have `smiles` (else `tpHeader`). A blank line is no record. JSON rows (`{"rows":[...]}`) take the same keys; a value is a string or a number, `parent` may be an array of names. The line of a CSV row is the line its record starts on (header = 1); of a JSON row, its 1-based index.

## What a row becomes

- A structure through the door of a SMILES text (`io::import`, coordinates computed), in a column to the right of the drawing, then moved by the layout.
- The **reserved data names**, in `atom.data` of the smallest atom of the structure, in this order, empty ones left out: `TP_ID` (the shown name), `MZ`, `RT`, `ADDUCT`, `AREA`. The values are written as typed (trimmed). These names are a **document convention** (the constant `tp_list::FIELDS`): mzLab's CLI reads the same names. **Deliberate deviation from the task prompt, which says they are reserved "like the four `MZLAB_*` fields":** they are not refused as user data by the data check (`doc::data_valid`), because SDF files written elsewhere carry fields of these names and must open; reserved by convention only.
- The **level** as the annotation (`ann`), on the scale of Schymanski et al. 2014, or on the scale for PFAS (Charbonnet et al. 2022) when the level is only in that one; a level of neither is kept as written.
- One **anchored arrow** per parent link (tp-01, `from` the smallest atom of the parent, `to` the smallest of the child).
- Then, in the same undo step: `scheme:orient` (the plan of `orient::plan`, applied when it gives turns; a refusal of that step does not stop the import: the structures stay as placed), the layout of `pathway:tree` (tp-05, with its routed arrows), the labels of the arrows (`pathway:label` of tp-07, `steplabel::plan` in the mode `pathway.label`: the formula delta; the mass modes stay refused until arch-web-17, and a refusal of the label step is a refusal of the import, with its key), and `TP<n>` for the rows with no name: the smallest numbers no structure holds, in the order of `pathway.number`, only for products (a nameless root stays nameless).
- A list with no parent link at all has no pathway: the structures stay in a column (no layout, no orientation) and the nameless rows are numbered in the order of the rows.

## `tp:import` and `tp:number`

`{"command":"tp:import","args":{"csv":TEXT}}` or `{"args":{"rows":[...]}}` (one of them; else `error.draw2.request`, exit 2). `tp:number` takes `{"force":BOOL}` (optional).

- **Atomic.** Every row is checked first. If any fails, nothing is applied and no `out` is written: exit 1, `draw-result/1` with `failed.stage` `command`, `failed.key` `error.draw2.tpRows` and `failed.rows` = `[{"line":n,"key":"error.draw2.tpRow..."}]` listing every failing row in line order, one key per row (the first that applies: shape, then structure, then name, number, adduct, level; duplicate, parent and cycle are listed when no earlier key took the row). Cycles: every row on a cycle (a row that is its own parent included) is `tpRowCycle`; a row that only descends from a cycle is not. A list refused as a whole: `tpHeader`, `tpEmpty`, `tpListSize` (above 10,000 rows, 1 MB or 15,000 atoms in all, counted in the text; this key has its own text, `tpSize` is the text of `tp:derive`), or the layout's key (`schemeSize` above 1000 linked structures, `schemeCycle`, `schemeDuplicate`: two rows listing the same parent twice are one arrow). A dry run validates and writes nothing.
- **`tp:number`** sets `TP_ID` = `TP1`, `TP2`, ... on the products of the pathway (the structures an anchored arrow reaches) in the order of the setting `pathway.number`: `layout` (default: by layer, then by position in the layer: the y of the box centre, the x with `pathway.dir=down`), `mz`, `rt` (a missing value last, then the smallest atom id). A root keeps its name. A name that is not `TP<n>` is kept unless `force`, and does not use up a number (TP1, kept, TP2: no gap). One undo step; no step when no name changes; a drawing with no anchored arrow is `error.draw2.schemeEmpty`.
- **Page.** The Add field and the file button take the CSV as they take a SMILES: a text whose first line is a header that starts with `smiles` and names only columns of the list is a list (`tp_list::looks_like`). One undo step. A refusal adds nothing, the live line says the first ten lines that failed (`draw2.say.tpRows`) and the error is `error.draw2.tpRows`. The style panel has the new choice "Number the TPs by" (`pathway.number`). The name is drawn under the structure next to the level: `TP3 · L2b` (`TP3` or `L2b` alone when only one is there), by `scene.rs`, from the data field `TP_ID` and the annotation; the same line is in the SVG.
- **Request table.** Rows `tp:import` and `tp:number` in the section `command` of `schema.rs`, `number` in the `pathway.*` row of `set`, `tp:import` and `tp:number` in the `commands` row (D13); `failed.rows` and the data names are described there and in this note. `docs/mzdraw.md` and `edit-schema.json` moved by that.

## Cost

- Native, release (`cargo test --release -p mzdraw --test tp_list -- --nocapture`): **200 rows 0.06 s** (kill test: under 2 s), 10,000 one-atom rows without a pathway 0.6 s, **15,000 atoms in 30 rows of 500 atoms 0.6 s**. Debug builds are 10 to 50 times slower. The total of 15,000 atoms is the cost's cap, not a format limit: the document finds an atom or a bond by scanning, so adding n atoms costs about n squared (measured in release: 10,000 atoms 0.1 s, 20,000 1.4 s, 30,000 5 s). That quadratic is in the shared document code (`Document::apply`) and is not changed here.
- **Sizes are read from the text before any structure is built** (`tp_list::atoms_in`: bracket atoms, `*`, element letters, `Cl` and `Br` as one, aromatic `b c n o p s`; an upper bound). The first version checked the caps after `io::import`, which is not linear in a row (a row of 16,000 atoms took 4.4 s, 900 KB did not finish); the review caught it. A row above 500 atoms or 5,000 characters is a row failure `tpRowSize` without being read; the atoms of the whole list (each row counted at most 500) above 15,000 is `tpListSize`. Tests that fail on the post-check version: `one_huge_row_is_refused_by_its_text...` (501, 16,000 and 900,000 atoms, bracket atoms, `Cl`, long text: each under 1 s, debug and release) and `many_large_rows_are_refused_by_the_total...` (3,000 rows of 400 atoms), and the growth test `the_import_grows_linearly_with_the_rows...` (400 and 800 rows, ratio under 3; `scheme:orient` reads at most 200 arrows, so the sizes are above its cap). The first version also had a scan of all atoms for each of 10,000 structures (13 s); the data of the structures are now read from one index.
- `scene.rs::levels` made one pass over all atoms for each structure (the cost of the live frame grew with structures times atoms); it is one pass in all now.

## Not built

- The table out (tp-09) and a table drawn on the canvas. `AREA` drawn as arrow width or dash.
- A convergent product is given by `;` in the `parent` column; there is no other syntax. A parent must be a typed `name` (a nameless row cannot be a parent). A multi-component SMILES (a salt) is refused (`tpRowSmiles`). A list is imported next to the drawing it finds: the layout of `pathway:tree` lays out every anchored arrow of the drawing, the existing ones included.
- Adduct validation beyond the syntax: the table of known adducts is chemistry and belongs to mzLab (arch-web-17, through the glue); `mzdraw` checks the form only and keeps the typed word. The m/z is not computed from the structure or checked against the adduct.
- **Equal molecules are not detected.** A list with 6 or more equal molecules freezes the page (the live structure check of the page takes a canonical form of equal components, found by tp-03); the import does not guard against it.
- The mass modes of `pathway.label` (`mass`, `delta+mass`, `name+delta`) stay refused (`pathway:label`'s own refusal) until arch-web-17: the import writes the formula delta.
- The line of a row refused for a quoted field that never closes is the last line of the text.
