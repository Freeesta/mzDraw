# Slice 3f, built (CML and Marvin documents)

one reader for CML (molecular convention) and MRV, the CML dialect of the Marvin documents (`crates/mzdraw/src/cml.rs`, on `xml.rs`; from the published convention, the MRV documentation and schema). In: every `molecule` with an `atomArray`, in the element form (`atom` children) or the array form (lists on `atomArray`), namespace prefixes or none; `elementType`, `x2` / `y2` (else `x3` / `y3`), `formalCharge`, `isotopeNumber` / `isotope`, `spinMultiplicity` / `radical`; the element `R` and `rgroupRef` are R groups, a dummy, a list or a pseudo atom is a query atom; orders `1 2 3`, `S D T`, `A` (kekulised), any other a query bond; `bondStereo` `W` / `H` from the first atom of its `atomRefs2` (else the bond's) and the MDL codes Marvin writes (1, 4, 6). Of a Marvin document also the reaction arrow, the reaction signs, the text boxes (their formatting in braces taken away) and the polylines with a head; its S-groups (nested molecules), R-group definitions and other graphics are counted and said. Names, formulas and properties of CML are passed over. Out (`save("cml")`): CML only, one `molecule` (abbreviations expanded, R groups as the element `R` with a label, wedges as `bondStereo`); CML has no place for arrows, plus signs and texts, so they are not written. Not read: `hydrogenCount` (the valence rules decide), `atomParity`, cis / trans `bondStereo` (the coordinates say it). Not stated in the pages read: the direction of y for `x2` / `y2`, taken upwards as in a molfile. MRV is not written.
