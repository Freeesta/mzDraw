# Enhanced-stereo groups kept, drawn, read and written, built (rdkit-compare 3 part 2)

Found by the RDKit comparison (`docs/study/rdkit-compare.md` F2, matrix #5 and #38; RDKit keeps `StereoGroup`s and draws them, `addStereoGroupAnnotation`): the groups of a CXSMILES (`&1`, `o1`, `a`) and of a V3000 molfile (`STEABS`, `STERACn`, `STERELn`) were dropped and said (part 1, PR #130, the import note `draw2.import.stereoGroups`). Now they are kept. This is the staged first step of the queued slices arch-std-12 (model, operation, drawing), arch-std-19 (files) and arch-std-20 (CXSMILES), with their names, so they build on it (decision of COO-MZDRAW, option (b)); what is left to them is listed in their rows of `docs/plan/queue.md` and under Not built.

**Document.** The atom field `stg` of `mzdraw/1` (no version change: an older editor keeps it as an unknown field and writes it back): `abs` (the absolute configuration), `andN` (the drawn isomer and its enantiomer in an unknown ratio, all the centres of group N together; `&N` of CXSMILES, `STERACn` of V3000) or `orN` (one of the two, unknown which; `oN`, `STERELn`), N from 1 to 2000 without a leading zero (`doc::stg_valid`). Code: `Atom::stg`, `Atom::set_stg`.

**Operation.** `{"op":"stereo.group","atoms":[2,4],"g":"and1"}` puts the atoms in one group; `"g":"none"` takes them out of any group. The semantics are the ones fixed with the mzLab chat for `draw_edit/1` (COO-MZDRAW): `g` is `abs`, `and<n>`, `or<n>` (n 1 to 2000, no leading zero) or `none`; the numbers are literal while a patch runs; at the END of the patch (`Document::settle_groups`, on the final record): a group that an operation of the patch put on an atom that is no defined tetrahedral centre (`Document::stereocentres`: the defined tetrahedral centres of `to_mol_stereo`, the wedges as drawn) fails the patch, all or none (`error.draw2.stereoGroup` with the atom); an atom that had a group and is no centre any more (a bond changed) leaves its group; then the groups of each kind are numbered 1, 2, ... in the order of their numbers (`and1` + `and3` becomes `and1` + `and2`). `none` never fails on an existing atom; an unknown atom is `error.draw2.noAtom`, an empty list or a word that is none (`&1`, `rac1`, `and0`, `and2001`, `or02`, the empty text) `error.draw2.stereoGroup`. One undo step; the inverse keeps the group each atom had, the drops and the renumbering included (`Inv::SetAtom`); in the property test "apply then undo returns the identical document" (`crates/mzdraw/tests/ops.rs`). mzLab logs the renumbered and dropped atoms as choices (`edit_group_renumbered`, `edit_group_dropped`); mzDraw has no choices list: the result of the patch is the document. The schema row of the operation is in `edit-schema.json` and the table of `docs/mzdraw.md`. CLI parity with mzLab's `mol draw` (the same operation) is pending COO 16's PR: not claimed here.

**Reading.** The groups `mzlab-mol` reads (CXSMILES tail, V3000 collections) are put on their atoms (`io::labelled`): `abs`, and the `and` and `or` groups numbered 1, 2, ... in the order of the record. No group is ever assumed (E20): a wedged centre outside every collection, and every centre of a V2000 file (a chiral flag, no collection), has none. A group on an atom that is no stereocentre of the drawing is not kept and is said (`draw2.import.stereoGroups`, which `mzlab-mol` makes rare: it refuses such a group). On the page and in the replay, the groups come in as `stereo.group` operations after the bonds (`editor::place_ops`), so the log replays them.

**Writing.** V3000: a `BEGIN COLLECTION` block before `END CTAB` (`STEABS`, then `STERACn`, then `STERELn`). SMILES: the CXSMILES tail (`|a:..,&1:..,o1:..|`, positions of the written atoms). In both, the number of a group is its rank among the groups of its kind in the drawing (a reader numbers them by their order): `and3` alone is written `&1` and read back `and1`. V2000, SDF, KET, CDXML, CML and RXN have no place for them: the loss manifest row `atom.stereoGroup` says it (`out loss:FORMAT`, `docs/mzdraw-formats.md`); V3000 and SMILES are "probe" (a group on an atom that is no stereocentre there is dropped by the reader).

**Drawing.** The group is drawn next to its atom, in the small size and the ink, by the same pass as the ids and the CIP labels (`scene::Painter::ids`: a free place round the atom, clear of the captions): `and1`, `or2`; `abs` only when the drawing also has an `and` or `or` group (a drawing whose centres are all absolute says nothing more than its wedges). In the canvas, the SVG, the PDF and the PNG.

**Changed goldens.** `result-envelope.json`: the four cases of part 1 with a group now keep it (no `imported` item; the SMILES and V3000 written back carry the group; their names say so). `smiles-files.json`: the alanine line with `&1:1` keeps its group (log, document, V3000, SMILES, SVG). `crates/mzdraw/tests/formats.rs` and `crates/mzdraw/tests/outcome.rs` asserted the dropped group: they now assert the kept group (on its atom, written back, no note), and the plumbing of `imported` is tested with the note of a name cut to 80 characters.

Tests: `crates/mzdraw/tests/stereo_groups.rs` (set, change and clear, each undone to the identical document, the log text; a patch judged at its end, the numbers compacted, a lost centre leaving its group, `none` never failing; refused words and atoms that are no stereocentres; CXSMILES and V3000 read and written back, the order and the numbers; no group assumed: a wedged centre outside the collections, V2000 with and without the chiral flag; the labels, `abs` only beside another kind; the formats that lose them, and the CLI log giving the page's bytes); `crates/mzdraw/tests/ops.rs` (the property test); golden `tests/data/log/stereo-groups.json` (four cases, three refused), wasm == native in `tests/parity.mjs`; e2e step "enhanced-stereo groups (rdkit-compare 3 part 2)" (keyboard, touch, pen: a CXSMILES typed in Add a structure, the labels in the SVG, the SMILES equals the golden). Screenshot at deviceScaleFactor 2: `docs/shots/rdkit-compare-3-stereo-groups.png` (written by the e2e step).

Not built (left to arch-std-12/19/20): the style `stereo_labels` (`words`: the IUPAC "and enantiomer" under a structure; `groups`; `off`) and the page's popover to set a group (the CLI route is `stereo.group`); KET flags; the V2000 chiral flag written as absolute, and its import notes; a chiral-flag label in the picture (study #38 stays partial).
