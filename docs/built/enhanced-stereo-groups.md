# Enhanced-stereo groups kept, drawn, read and written, built (rdkit-compare 3 part 2)

Found by the RDKit comparison (`docs/study/rdkit-compare.md` F2, matrix #5 and #38; RDKit keeps `StereoGroup`s and draws them, `addStereoGroupAnnotation`): the groups of a CXSMILES (`&1`, `o1`, `a`) and of a V3000 molfile (`STEABS`, `STERACn`, `STERELn`) were dropped and said (part 1, PR #130, the import note `draw2.import.stereoGroups`). Now they are kept. This is the staged first step of the queued slices arch-std-12 (model, operation, drawing), arch-std-19 (files) and arch-std-20 (CXSMILES), with their names, so they build on it (decision of COO-MZDRAW, option (b)); what is left to them is listed in their rows of `docs/plan/queue.md` and under Not built.

**Document.** The atom field `stg` of `mzdraw/1` (no version change: an older editor keeps it as an unknown field and writes it back): `abs` (the absolute configuration), `andN` (the drawn isomer and its enantiomer in an unknown ratio, all the centres of group N together; `&N` of CXSMILES, `STERACn` of V3000) or `orN` (one of the two, unknown which; `oN`, `STERELn`), N from 1 to 2000 in ASCII digits without a leading zero (`doc::stg_valid`: `and+1`, `and 1`, `and\u{661}` are no group, though `str::parse` would take the sign). Code: `Atom::stg`, `Atom::set_stg`.

**Operation.** `{"op":"stereo.group","atoms":[2,4],"g":"and1"}` puts the atoms in one group; `"g":"none"` takes them out of any group. The semantics are the ones fixed with the mzLab chat for `draw_edit/1` (COO-MZDRAW): `g` is `abs`, `and<n>`, `or<n>` (n 1 to 2000, no leading zero) or `none`; the numbers are literal while a patch runs; at the END of the patch (`Document::settle_groups`, on the final record): a group that an operation of the patch put on an atom that is no defined tetrahedral centre (`Document::stereocentres`: the defined tetrahedral centres of `to_mol_stereo`, the wedges as drawn) fails the patch, all or none (`error.draw2.stereoGroup` with the atom); an atom that had a group and is no centre any more (a bond changed) leaves its group; then the groups of each kind are numbered 1, 2, ... in the order of their numbers (`and1` + `and3` becomes `and1` + `and2`). `none` never fails on an existing atom; an unknown atom is `error.draw2.noAtom`, an empty list or a word that is none (`&1`, `rac1`, `and0`, `and2001`, `or02`, `and+1`, `and 1`, a digit that is not ASCII, the empty text) `error.draw2.stereoGroup`. One undo step; the inverse keeps the group each atom had, the drops and the renumbering included (`Inv::SetAtom`); in the property test "apply then undo returns the identical document" (`crates/mzdraw/tests/ops.rs`: the general one, and one on a drawing with four wedged centres whose random steps set groups, have them compacted and dropped, each step undone and redone to the identical document, with the counts of each path asserted). mzLab logs the renumbered and dropped atoms as choices (`edit_group_renumbered`, `edit_group_dropped`); mzDraw has no choices list: the result of the patch is the document. The schema row of the operation is in `edit-schema.json` and the table of `docs/mzdraw.md`. CLI parity: mzLab's `draw_edit/1` has the same operation with the same shape and rules (mzLab main `b468b69f7c8c62e71e3f25f956be3e18aa330172`); the pinned `mzlab-mol` of this repository is older, so the engine side of `mzlab-cli mol draw` comes with a later pin bump (rule 9: a PR of its own).

**Reading.** The groups `mzlab-mol` reads (CXSMILES tail, V3000 collections) are put on their atoms (`io::labelled`): `abs`, and the `and` and `or` groups numbered 1, 2, ... in the order of the record. No group is ever assumed (E20): a wedged centre outside every collection, and every centre of a V2000 file (a chiral flag, no collection), has none. A group on an atom that is no stereocentre of the drawing is not kept and is said (`draw2.import.stereoGroups`), and the numbers left are compacted as at the end of a patch (`Document::compact_groups`, the same function): a read document never holds `and2` without `and1`. `mzlab-mol` makes the case rare: its CXSMILES reader refuses such a group, and its V3000 reader takes an atom that is no defined centre out of its collection (a collection left empty is no group) without a word, so that drop is not said here (a fix belongs to mzLab). On the page and in the replay, the groups come in as `stereo.group` operations after the bonds (`editor::place_ops`), so the log replays them.

**Writing.** V3000: a `BEGIN COLLECTION` block before `END CTAB` (`STEABS`, then `STERACn`, then `STERELn`). SMILES: the CXSMILES tail (`|a:..,&1:..,o1:..|`, positions of the written atoms). In both, the number of a group is its rank among the groups of its kind in the drawing (a reader numbers them by their order): `and3` alone is written `&1` and read back `and1`. V2000, SDF, KET, CDXML, CML and RXN have no place for them: the loss manifest row `atom.stereoGroup` says it (`out loss:FORMAT`, `docs/mzdraw-formats.md`); V3000 and SMILES are "probe" (a group on an atom that is no stereocentre there is dropped by the reader).

**Diff and hunks.** `diff::diff` writes a changed group as `stereo.group` on that atom with the literal number of the second document (`none` for a group taken away). A hunk unites the ids its operations touch, so the group of an atom and a wedge from that atom (a bond whose end it is) fall in one hunk: a subset of the hunks never applies the group without the wedge that makes the atom a centre (`a_subset_of_the_hunks_of_a_diff_keeps_a_group_with_its_wedge`). A patch built by hand that keeps the group's operation and leaves out its wedge fails whole, `error.draw2.stereoGroup` (the rule of the end of a patch), and changes nothing; a subset that leaves out a lower-numbered group of the same kind gets its numbers compacted at its end (`and2` alone becomes `and1`).

**Cost.** `Document::settle_groups` reads the centres again (`stereocentres`, a molfile round trip) only for the pieces the patch touched (the atoms and bonds its inverse names, and the atoms it set a group on; `Document::touched_pieces`, a union-find over the atoms' places) and only when such a piece holds a group; an atom of another piece keeps its group, judged by the last patch that changed its piece. On 25 structures of eight atoms with all 50 centres in groups, one move of an atom costs about 4 times the same move on the drawing without groups in a release build and about 7 times in a debug build (the least CPU time of 15 runs taken in turns; the whole-drawing read was 19 and 39 times). `the_check_at_the_end_of_a_patch_costs_a_bounded_multiple_of_a_plain_edit` asserts at most 10 times (a CPU-time ratio, no wall-clock bound). Most of what is left is the round trip of the touched piece itself.

**Drawing.** The group is drawn next to its atom, in the small size and the ink, by the same pass as the ids and the CIP labels (`scene::Painter::ids`: a free place round the atom, clear of the captions): `and1`, `or2`; `abs` only when the drawing also has an `and` or `or` group (a drawing whose centres are all absolute says nothing more than its wedges). In the canvas, the SVG, the PDF and the PNG.

**Changed goldens.** `result-envelope.json`: the four cases of part 1 with a group now keep it (no `imported` item; the SMILES and V3000 written back carry the group; their names say so). `smiles-files.json`: the alanine line with `&1:1` keeps its group (log, document, V3000, SMILES, SVG). `crates/mzdraw/tests/formats.rs` and `crates/mzdraw/tests/outcome.rs` asserted the dropped group: they now assert the kept group (on its atom, written back, no note), and the plumbing of `imported` is tested with the note of a name cut to 80 characters.

Tests: `crates/mzdraw/src/io.rs` (a group dropped on read leaves no gap, and the ranks written); `crates/mzdraw/tests/stereo_groups.rs` (set, change and clear, each undone to the identical document, the log text; a patch judged at its end, the numbers compacted, a lost centre leaving its group, `none` never failing; refused words and atoms that are no stereocentres, a sign, a space or a digit that is not ASCII; only the touched pieces judged, a group gone anywhere compacting the rest; a V3000 collection dropped on read leaving no gap; the hunks of a diff keeping a group with its wedge; the cost ratio; CXSMILES and V3000 read and written back, the order and the numbers; no group assumed: a wedged centre outside the collections, V2000 with and without the chiral flag; the labels, `abs` only beside another kind; the formats that lose them, and the CLI log giving the page's bytes); `crates/mzdraw/tests/ops.rs` (the property test); golden `tests/data/log/stereo-groups.json` (four cases, three refused), wasm == native in `tests/parity.mjs`; e2e step "enhanced-stereo groups (rdkit-compare 3 part 2)" (keyboard, touch, pen: a CXSMILES typed in Add a structure, the labels in the SVG, the SMILES equals the golden).
