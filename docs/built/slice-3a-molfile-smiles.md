# Slice 3a, built (formats: molfile and SMILES)

out, `Editor::save` (one door for the page, the replay request `out` and the CLI): `molfile` (V2000; V3000 above 999 atoms or bonds), `molfile-v3000`, `smiles`, next to `mzdraw`, `svg`, `log`. The SMILES is the one `mzlab-mol` writes (Kekulé, stereo from the wedges as drawn, pieces joined by `.`) with the brackets a reader does not need taken away (`CC(=O)O`, not `[CH3][C](=[O])[OH]`); an R group is `[*]` / `[*:n]`. The V3000 file carries the wedges as `CFG` and the R groups as `R#` with `RGROUPS` (the V3000 text is the one of `mzlab-mol`, marked line by line). In: a molfile or an SDF, V2000 or V3000, every record side by side (64 at a time, the rest is said); SMILES, one or one per line with names; the R groups of all three forms come back as labels. Out of scope parts open and are listed in words under the Add field, never dropped in silence (`Imported::notes`): query atoms (read as unnumbered R groups), query bonds (read as single bonds), S-groups (left out), enhanced-stereo groups "and"/"or" (the centres keep their wedges, the grouping is not kept). A file whose coordinates do not fit a document is refused. Not read yet: the data fields of an SDF (`> <name>`), atom aliases, the atom lists themselves.
