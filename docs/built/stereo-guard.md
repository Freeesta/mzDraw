# Stereo guard, built

(plan-21; `crates/mzdraw/src/stereo_guard.rs`, hooks in `editor.rs` (`run`, `run_guarded`, `say_stereo`), `editor_nudge.rs` and `commands.rs`; `outcome.rs` arm `stereo`; section "Replay result" of docs/mzdraw.md.) In mzDraw the wedges are the drawing and the configuration is derived at export, so moving, turning or mirroring atoms can change a configuration with no stereo edit.

**Elements, not pieces.** A configuration is kept per stereo element: a stereocentre (the narrow end of a wedge) or a double bond that has something on each end. The signature of an element is the canonical isomeric SMILES `mzlab-mol` writes (`to_mol_stereo`, then `Mol::canonical`, the path of `save("smiles")`) from the element alone: the core atoms with their neighbours, every neighbour made distinct by its isotope. So "this centre is mirrored" is said whatever else happens to its piece (a substituent added, two pieces joined, an atom removed). Compared on the neighbours the element still has (at least three for a centre). A change that leaves the same atoms forming the same molecule (the mirror image of a meso compound) is no change: the canonical text of the whole piece is compared, only when something was flagged. `signature(&Document, &[Piece])` (canonical SMILES of the pieces that hold a wedge or a double bond) and `pieces` stay as the piece-level API.

**Live line** (page, editor commands, key nudges: `Editor::run`, `run_guarded`, `nudge` and `rotate` steps). Before a step that has a `move`, `rotate` or `flip` (alone or with other operations), the elements near what moves are copied; after it they are compared. A change is said by the live line as `draw2.say.stereoChanged` ("Configuration changed at atoms {what}.", the atoms of the elements that changed, nothing else) and is also kept in `Editor::notice` (`state().notice`), which the page paints in the visible status line beside the counts until the next step or an undo (the paragraph `#d2-live` stays `aria-live`). Report only: the step stands; undo reverts it. The words are chosen in Rust; the page only paints `notice`. Nothing is computed when the document has no wedge and no double bond (one pass over the bonds), when no element is near what moves, or for a rigid move or turn of a whole piece; never while a drag is under way (the drag is one `move` at the release). A `log` of a request carries operations only and says no sentence; its verdict is the arm below.

**Request arm `{"stereo":"kept"}`** (row `expect stereo` of `schema::REQUEST`; goldens `tests/data/log/stereo-guard.json`, parity wasm == native): every element of the drawing after `import` is compared with the end. False when an element is arranged otherwise, lost its wedge, or is gone (an atom removed, fewer than three neighbours left): a configuration that is not there is not kept (a deliberate deviation from the plan, which only compared what was still there; pinned by the golden cases "a centre that loses a neighbour is gone" and "a mirror then the methyl removed"). `detail`: "the configuration changed at atoms …". A mirror followed by any structure edit is still said (tests `a_mirror_cannot_be_hidden_by_a_structure_edit`).

**Budget** (release build, median of 20, `cargo run --release -p mzdraw --example stereo_bench`, machine dependent): one signature of a piece of 100 atoms with 10 wedges 0.3 to 0.6 ms over runs here (limit 1 ms; a reviewer measured 0.59 ms), a whole guarded mirror of that piece 0.43 ms. A step on a 20,000-atom drawing without wedge or double bond: one pass over the bonds (a test bounds it). The signature counter `stereo_guard::computed()` is one relaxed atomic add per signature (nothing measurable, no thread in the wasm build); the tests need it in the library. Module size (`tools/build_site.sh`): 873,610 B on main (3e20565) and 902,656 B with this slice, +29,046 B: over the 20 kB the plan asked for (the element code and its copies of the neighbourhood; the first, per-piece version was +13,575 B). The cap (4,000,000 B) is untouched; the PR says so and leaves the call to the coordinator.

**Proof.** `crates/mzdraw/tests/stereo_guard.rs`: plain flip flagged, rigid move and turn never; a neighbour dragged across the others; only the changed centre named; a double bond moved across named by its own atoms (with a wedge elsewhere too); the arm, the structure-edit bypasses, meso and E/Z; kill test of 5,000 edits on 1,000 random drawings (one or two centres, a ring, a double bond, a meso compound; whole and partial flips, rigid moves and turns, drags of one atom): the guard agrees with the canonical signature of the piece every time, no rigid edit is flagged (rates printed by the test). `stereo_guard_drag.rs`: the counter (nothing during a drag, nothing for a rigid edit). Independent: `tools/fidelity/stereo.py` (RDKit, fetched at run time) compares our verdict with the change of the InChI stereo layer (`/b`, `/t`, `/m`, `/s`) of the molfile of the drawing for the 58 stereo structures of `tests/data/fidelity/set.smi` (centres and double bonds), a flip and a drag each: 116 of 116 agree (RDKit 2026.09.2, 2026-11-21, `tests/data/fidelity/stereo.json`; `stereo_guard.rs` replays the same edits and checks the file is current). e2e: mouse, keyboard, finger and pen, the visible text asserted; screenshot `docs/screenshots/plan-21-stereo-guard.png`.

**Not built / not proven:** re-solving the wedge placement (IUPAC ST-1.1), atropisomers, allenes, enhanced-stereo groups, cumulenes; `atoms.xy` is not an operation of this version; a pseudo-centre (two equal substituents) has no configuration to InChI but is read as an element here, and the piece-level check hides the flag only when the whole molecule is unchanged; a configuration `mzlab-mol` cannot read from the drawing has no text; the drag proof mirrors one neighbour through the centre or across the bond, not every drag; the exit codes of `mzlab-cli` are mzLab's.
