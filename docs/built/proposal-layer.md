# An agent's proposal shown as a layer, built (plan-16)

`crates/mzdraw/src/proposal.rs`. `Editor::propose(ops)` holds a `draw_edit/1` patch apart from the drawing: the document, the log and the undo history do not change. The hunks are those of `diff::hunks` (plan-15), numbered from 1. A patch must apply to the drawing as it is (checked once on a copy), have 1 to 2000 operations (`error.draw2.proposalEmpty`, `error.draw2.proposalSize`); a new proposal replaces the old one (no merging).

- `accept_hunk(n)`: one undo step with exactly the operations of the hunk (no route operation follows, so the log carries what the agent wrote). `reject_hunk(n)` drops it. `accept_all` runs every open hunk, in order, as ONE undo step; `reject_all` drops what is left. The remaining hunks stay valid (plan-15). A number that is not open is `error.draw2.proposalHunk`; no proposal, `error.draw2.noProposal`.
- Stale: any other change of the history (an edit, an undo, a redo) ends the proposal for good (its hunks were written against another drawing); it is no longer drawn or acted on. An undo of an accept does not bring the hunk back.
- Scene: added atoms, bonds and objects in the accent colour (`colour::ACCENT`, token `--mzd-accent`), removed ones struck in the warning colour, changed ones outlined, moved atoms drawn where they would stand with a line from where they are, each hunk numbered (the current one has a second ring). Built from the operations with one table of positions (`Mark`, `Overlay::marks`), not looked up per mark.
- Keys (on the canvas, only while a proposal is open; arch-std-06's command table does not exist yet, so these are fixed): `]` and `[` next and previous hunk (round the ring), Enter accepts the hunk with the keyboard, Delete or Backspace rejects it, with Ctrl all of them. A tap or click on a hunk number gives it the keyboard (the rest of that press is swallowed: the tool does not act). The live line says where the hunk is and what it does, in words from its operations (`draw2.hunk.*`: the ids of the atoms, bonds or objects added, removed, changed, moved).
- Page: a panel over the canvas lists the open hunks with Show, Accept, Reject, and Accept all / Reject all; the patch is given in the field "A patch to review" of the structure palette. `window.MZDRAW.propose(text)` and `proposal(action)` are the calls a host can make (the element API of arch-web-02 does not exist yet). State: `state().proposal` `{cur, hunks: [{n, say: [[key, what]]}]}` or null.

CLI twin: the replay request takes `"propose": PATCH` and `"accept": [hunk numbers]` (rows of the `request` table of `schema.rs`, `docs/mzdraw.md`). The log then holds exactly the operations of the accepted hunks, in the order given; `[]` or no `accept` leaves drawing and log as they were. A refusal is a keyed error (exit 2 as every refusal of a bare request) or, with `report`, `dry_run` or `expect`, a verdict `failed.stage` `proposal` (new value, in `docs/schemas/draw-result-1.json`; `op` is the index in `accept`, none for a refused `propose`). `accept` without `propose`, or a number that is not a positive integer, is an invalid request. No draw-result/1 key is new, no `draw_edit/1` operation or field.

Tests: `crates/mzdraw/tests/proposal.rs` (hunks 2 and 4 of four accepted gives `a` plus those hunks, two undos give `a`; reject all leaves document and log; keys; tap on a number; marks; words; refusals; stale; 2000 hunks held, drawn and accepted in under 3 s, a patch of 2001 refused in under 0.5 s); `tests/ops.rs` (random pairs: any order of any subset accepted hunk by hunk equals `Session::run` of the same hunks, one undo step each); `tests/data/log/proposal.json` (cases, results, refused; wasm == native by `tests/parity.mjs`); e2e: mouse and keyboard, touch and pen, screenshots `docs/shots/plan16-proposal-*.png` at deviceScaleFactor 2.

Independent proof: `tests/parity.mjs` takes the hunks from the `diff` door of two documents made by plain replay (no `propose` in the path), replays the accepted hunks' operations as a plain log and requires the same document and log bytes as the request with `propose` + `accept`, native and wasm. It reads our outputs; it does not reuse the proposal code. The review-time study is a person's experiment: `docs/plan/velocity-protocol.md`, last section; nothing is claimed from it.

Not built, said plainly: merging two proposals; an agent of our own and any network; bringing a hunk back after an undo; rebinding the keys (arch-std-06); the element API (arch-web-02); a proposal that survives an edit of the person; hunk marks for `rotate`/`flip`/`ring` operations beyond a ring on the atoms they name; the time study (nobody has run it).
