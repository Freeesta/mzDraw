# Arrow labels computed from the two structures (tp-07), built

(`crates/mzdraw/src/steplabel.rs`; command wiring `commands.rs`, `editor_pathway.rs`; room for labels in `pathway.rs`; true minus in `scene.rs`; tests `crates/mzdraw/tests/steplabel.rs`, `crates/mzdraw-wasm/tests/glue.rs`; golden `tests/data/log/pathway-label.json`; page: `web/palettes.js`, `web/mzdraw.js`, `web/lang/mzdraw.js`, two e2e steps; proof `tools/fidelity/delta.py` with `tools/fidelity/delta.reference.json`; screenshots `docs/screenshots/tp-07-*.png`.) No `draw_edit/1` op or field is new: the command writes `obj.set` of `text`.

**Command.** `{"command":"pathway:label","args":{"force":bool,"isomer":WORD}}` (both optional), one undo step; the button "Label arrows" (objects palette, `pathway-label`) and the canvas menu item `pathway.label` run the same Rust. For each arrow anchored at both ends to two different structures it writes the formula delta `T - P`, in the spelling of `mzlab_mol::delta::print` (`+O`, `-CH2`, `-Cl+OH`), on the Hill counts of the record (implicit hydrogens included, as `outline.rs` counts). Equal formulas get the isomer word of the caller. "isomer" means equal formula only: stereoisomers, tautomers and even identical structures all get it (`isomer`; the page passes the catalog text `draw2.label.isomer`, the CLI default is `isomer`). Counting is one linear pass over the record (`Document::to_mol`, which appends the atoms an abbreviation such as Ph, OMe or Boc expands to): the structures are the connected pieces of the record's bonds, an R group is the element `R`. The charge is not part of a delta (`delta::parse` reads elements only): acetic acid to acetate is `-H`, acetate to acetic acid `+H`; two structures with the same atoms and another net charge are left alone and counted (`skipped`), never called an isomer. An atom with a free-text label (neither a table abbreviation nor an R group; the record holds a helium stand-in for it, which must never read as `+He`) makes its arrow skipped too; a valid document cannot hold such a label, so this is a guard, tested on a document built in memory. Data holds ASCII `-`. The painter alone turns the `-` of a token that is a signed delta into U+2212 (`steplabel::display`, in the arrow text of `scene.rs`, so the SVG and the PDF drawn from the display list show it); the document, the log, the molfile, CDXML, KET and the other exports keep the ASCII `-`. The e2e golden has a minus delta (`-O`) and asserts both forms.

**Whose label it is, no stored flag.** The text is split on white space. It is rewritten only if empty or if every token is one this command writes: a signed delta (a sign `+`, `-` or U+2212, then what `delta::parse` reads, at least one element), a signed number with exactly 4 decimals, the isomer word. `hydroxylation (CYP)`, `H2O`, `O3`, `NaBH4`, `+O, 37 °C` stay; `force` rewrites all (tested). The isomer word is an argument with a default constant (deterministic; the page forwards its catalog word): a word of another language than the caller's is the person's text and is kept (never loses text), and a word with white space (or empty, or over 40 characters) is refused as `error.draw2.request`. `below` (arch-web-13) is not on `main` and an object text allows no line break (a document that has one is refused on import), so two tokens would share one line (`+O +15.9949`); `owned` accepts the two-line form `+O` newline `+15.9949` as ours (tested) for the slice that adds `below`.

**Live line and report.** `draw2.say.labelled` (labelled, kept, skipped) and `draw2.say.labelNone`; `changed` is reported by `draw-result/1` (`commands::reports_change`); an unchanged text is no operation and no undo step.

**Setting** `pathway.label` = `delta` (default, not written to a settings file) | `mass` | `delta+mass` | `name+delta`, accepted by `--set` and the settings file. The page offers no select for it (it is not in the schema `choices`): the other three words always fail until they can run, and a select of words that fail is not shown.

**Refusals** (exit 1, `failed.stage` `command`, nothing changes): `error.draw2.labelMass` for `mass` and `delta+mass`; (`name+delta` was refused with `error.draw2.labelSteps` here; tp-10 built it, see `tp-10-named-steps.md`); `error.draw2.schemeSize` above `pathway::MAX_ARROWS` (1000) anchored arrows or `steplabel::MAX_ATOMS` (100,000) atoms, checked before any work; an invalid argument is `error.draw2.request`. Cost: the record and its pieces (union-find) are built once, the arrows are read in one pass over the objects, the counts of a structure once however many arrows end in it; `layout::pieces` is not called. The layout builds the arrow text widths in one pass over the objects (not one lookup per edge). Tests time `steplabel::plan` alone (the document layer owns the application of the operations, which scans for an object by id): 1000 arrows against 250 (ratio bound 9 for a 4-fold size, with a floor), and 1000 arrows with 50,000 other objects listed before them (bound 6 times, with a floor); both fail on the two mutations tried, a `layout::pieces` call and a `doc.object(id)` per arrow, in debug and in release.

**Room for labels (tp-05 step 5).** `pathway::Edge::label` is the width of the arrow text as painted; the gap between two columns is raised until the visible arrow is at least that long (`GapNote::rule` `label`, after `slope` and `length`). No golden of tp-05 held an arrow text, so none moved; `tests/data/log/pathway-label.json` has the case with labels (a long text widens the gap, a short one does not).

**Request rows** (`schema::REQUEST`): command `pathway:label` (args `force`, `isomer`; the keys `error.draw2.labelMass` is in its row (`error.draw2.labelSteps` is gone: tp-10)), setting `pathway.label`, and the name `pathway:label` added to the text of the `commands` row. `docs/mzdraw.md` and `tests/data/log/edit-schema.json` moved by that, `context-menu.json` by the new menu item (blessed).

**Proof.** `python3 tools/fidelity/delta.py` reads OUR replay output (the labelled document and its molfile), gives each structure to RDKit (`CalcMolFormula`, `ExactMolWt`) and compares the formula difference of the two ends of each arrow with the label we wrote: 15 of 15 of our labels agree (RDKit 2026.9.2, 2026-11-21; the labels that are the person's text are not compared); the RDKit mass shifts are written to the reference file only. What it does not show: the mass labels (not built), anything about charged structures, R groups or the drawing. `tools/fidelity/delta.py` compares 15 of our labels from four schemes, among them abbreviations (Ph, OMe, Boc), a ring with a heteroatom (piperidine), a charged pair (acetic acid and acetate) and an isomer, and fails if fewer than 15 labels were compared (0 of 0 is not a pass). The mass shifts of the study (`+O` 15.9949, `-CH2` -14.0157, `+C6H8O6` 176.0321, `+SO3` 79.9568, `-H2` -2.0157, `+H2O` 18.0106) are checked in `crates/mzdraw-wasm/tests/glue.rs` against the masses `sel` returns (not typed ones) to 1e-4; `+C6H8O6` is the mass of ascorbic acid, not a difference of two structures.

**Follow-up (not in this PR).** Applying `obj.set` in the document layer scans the objects once per operation: 1000 arrows with 50,000 other objects cost about 0.30 s there against 9 ms for the labelling itself. It is bounded by the caps (1000 arrows, 100,000 atoms), not a hang; an id index in the document layer would remove it.

**Not built.** The mass modes (`mass`, `delta+mass`): the numbers exist (`sel`, `label.mass`, in `crates/mzdraw-wasm/src/glue.rs`, feature `sel`), but the core has no callback to the glue, so the command is refused with `error.draw2.labelMass`; backlog row **tp-07b: mass modes via a glue arm** (rounding half to even at 4 decimals goes there). The `below` slot (arch-web-13), screenshots of `delta+mass`. The property test runs on 50 seeded random chain pairs built here (no tp-03 patches): `delta::add(P, label)` equals `T` on the outline's Hill formulas. The kill test (a person's label rewritten) did not fire in any e2e or synthetic case.
