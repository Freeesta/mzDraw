# Palettes and export defaults in the settings, built (R1, Rust only; the page does not use them yet)

`crates/mzdraw/src/palettes.rs` holds where the palettes of the page are (`docs/mzdraw-ui.md` §4-§5). There are 16 palettes, each docked on an edge or floating, with an order along its edge, collapsed or hidden. The presets are desktop, tablet and phone, chosen by the width of the viewport. The user's changes are kept field by field over the preset, in the object `layout` of `mzdraw-settings/1`, and only the changes are written. Unknown keys and palette ids are kept. The rest is decided in Rust: laying out rows and columns that wrap, a 12 px snap to edges and to other palettes, docking where a palette is dropped (a row at the top or bottom, a column at the sides), moving with the arrow keys, Enter (dock or float), Escape (back), keeping every palette inside the viewport, and the key `\` (hide or show every palette, flag `PALETTES` = 128). The object `export` holds `default` (one of 12 formats, `svg` first) and `background` (`white` or `none`), and is not yet applied to the files. `Editor::set` takes `export.<key>=`, `layout.<preset|snap|reset>=` and `layout=<object>`; `set_style` with a settings file reads both objects, and a bad one is refused (`error.draw2.layout`, `error.draw2.export`). In wasm: `palettes(w, h, sizes)`, `palette(action, id, x, y, shift)`, `export_settings()`; `style()` now writes the whole settings file. Covered by `tests/palettes.rs`, including a property test over 60 random sequences: every palette stays inside the viewport and the settings round-trip. Page input that is not a finite number is refused (a viewport is kept within 100,000 px), so every position written is one the reader takes; keys of a palette entry this version does not know are kept; a key on a palette held by the pointer is ignored; a palette docked on a full edge goes into its row or column by (row or column, place along it), and the hidden palettes of that edge are numbered after the shown ones. `f64::clamp` with bounds known only at run time is avoided (`within`): its check links the formatting of floats (22,762 B of module, measured). Rule 15: the replay request sets `layout=` and `export.default/background` through the route of `Editor::set` (`set`, the `--set` of the CLI) and returns the settings file with `out: settings` (`Editor::save("settings")`); the `settings` cases of `tests/data/log/settings.json` hold wasm == native parity for them (`tests/parity.mjs`), and two refused settings are recorded.
