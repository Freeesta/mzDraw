# CIP labels in the picture, built (rdkit-compare 6)

Found by the RDKit comparison (`docs/study/rdkit-compare.md` matrix #37; RDKit `addStereoAnnotation`): the engine `cip` of mzLab gave R/S and E/Z through the glue (`replay_glue`), but nothing drew them. The setting `display.cip` (`off` by default, `on`) draws them in brackets: `(R)`, `(S)` at a stereocentre, `(E)`, `(Z)` beside a double bond.

**Engine, not core.** The core crate has no CIP code and must not get one: it is given a labeller, `cip::Labeller` (a plain function pointer, as the masses are given a `values::Measurer`), held in `Editor::labeller`. The bridge crate gives it `glue::cip_labels`: `cip/1` of mzLab on the drawing's molfile, its 0-based atom and bond numbers turned into document ids (`glue::ids`); an atom of an expanded abbreviation (a number past the drawing's own atoms) and a bond whose two atoms are not one bond of the drawing have no label. The labels are computed once per change of the drawing (`Editor::cip_labelled`, cached by the generation of the session) and only when the setting is on.

**Structure by structure, with caps.** The engine's work grows exponentially with the open stereo sites of what it is given (a record of 8 copies of `CC(C)C=CC(=O)NC1CCC(CC1)C=CC` given whole took 108 s in a debug build). `cip::label` gives it each connected structure on its own (a document of the structure's atoms and bonds, with their ids) and joins the labels, so the work is the sum of the structures' (8 copies: 25 ms, 40 copies: 127 ms, debug). A structure with more than `cip::MAX_OPEN` (16) open sites (`cip::open_sites`, from the drawing alone: a C, N, P, S or Si centre with three or more single bonds and no wedge or hash starting at it, and a double bond drawn "either") or more than `cip::MAX_ATOMS` (500) atoms is not given to the engine; a structure the engine refuses has no labels. Neither is silent: the counts are `draw2.export.cipCapped` and `draw2.export.cipFailed`, in `lost` of a picture (svg, pdf, png) on the CLI and in the export note of the page, and the page's live line says them once when they appear (`cip_notes`). A centre the drawing leaves undefined has no label (the engine labels what is defined).

**Routes.** The page has the labeller (`mzdraw-wasm` `new_page`). The CLI: `replay::replay_engines(request, n, Engines { measure, cip })` (`replay_with` keeps its signature: it is `replay_engines` with no labeller); the glue replay (`cargo run -p mzdraw-wasm --example replay_glue`, `glue::replay` with no `engines`) passes both engines, so `{"import":..., "set":["display.cip=on"], "out":"svg"}` draws the labels. The plain core replay (`--example replay`) has no engine: it draws no label and its `draw-result/1` says so in `lost` (`draw2.export.cipEngine`, for a picture: svg, pdf, png): no label is made up.

**Setting.** `--set display.cip=on`, key `cip` of the settings file (written only when on), `Editor::get("display.cip")`, the word list in `settings::schema_json`, a schema row in the section `set` (`edit-schema.json` and the table of `docs/mzdraw.md` re-blessed); a word that is none is `error.draw2.styleValue`. A row of the Preferences (group Display), texts `draw2.prefs.display.cip.*`; Reset of the group puts it back to `off`.

**Drawing.** The labels are notes of the painter, placed by the same pass as the ids (`scene::Painter::ids`, `id_spot`): an atom's label on one of 16 places round the atom, a bond's on either side of its middle, each where it is farthest from what is drawn near it and from the notes placed before it; in the small size and the ink. The pass now does nothing at all when there is no id and no label to place (it used to take the label test of every atom). Exports: `svg::to_svg_full`, `pdf::to_pdf_full`, `png::plan_full` take the labels (`to_svg_measured`, `to_pdf_with`, `plan_with` keep their signatures and draw none).

**Checked against RDKit.** The labels the glue gives agree with RDKit 2026.09.2 `rdCIPLabeler.AssignCIPLabels` on the five structures of the goldens and the comparison set tried (`C/C=C/C[C@H](N)C(=O)O`, a cyclohexane with two centres, threonine-like, a pyranose with five centres, `F/C=C\Cl`), by a run with the RDKit of the comparison (not a test: RDKit is not a dependency).

Tests: `crates/mzdraw/tests/display_cip.rs` (one call per structure for 8 and 40 structures; a capped and a refused structure counted, said on the page and in `lost`; a labeller made in the test: off by default, drawn in brackets in the SVG, the PDF and the PNG; a label by its atom and by its bond; the labeller runs once per change of the drawing; without a labeller no label and the `lost` item; the settings file); `crates/mzdraw-wasm/tests/cip.rs` (the real engine: 40 structures cost at most ten times 8, by CPU time; a structure past the cap has no label and the glue door says it in `lost`; R and S, E and Z by document ids, no label without stereo; the glue door draws them and an editor with the glue's labeller gives the same bytes; the golden file); `crates/mzdraw/tests/prefs.rs` (the reset list); golden `tests/data/log/cip.json` (section `values` through the glue door, wasm == native in `tests/parity.mjs`; a core `results` case with the `lost` item; a refused word); e2e step "display cip (rdkit-compare 6)" (keyboard, touch, pen; the page equals the glue golden, Reset hides them). Screenshots at deviceScaleFactor 2, written by the e2e step under these names: `docs/shots/rdkit-compare-6-display-cip-prefs.png`, `-canvas.png`.

Not built: italic labels; `showAllCIPCodes`-style labels on centres the drawing does not define (the engine labels what is defined); a CIP label in a file format (CDXML has no field written here).

Pin bump 3: `cip/1` now works per piece and refuses a piece past its own work limit (`error.cip.tooComplex`), which is counted `failed` like any engine error; the caps above stay (`docs/built/pin-bump-3.md`).
