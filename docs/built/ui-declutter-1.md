# UI declutter 1: a lighter page

The owner tried the live page (dark theme, 2000x1130 and a phone-width viewport) and asked for six changes. All are UI only: the layout rule and the theme default are Rust, the page paints and forwards events.

## What it does
1. **Light by default.** `ui.theme` defaults to `light` (`prefs.rs`: `default_of`, `Ui::default`; the settings file writes `theme` only when it is not `light`). A first visit, with nothing stored and even on a system that prefers dark, opens the light theme before the first paint (`web/theme.js` puts `light` on the root when nothing is stored). `dark` and `auto` (the system, or the host that set `data-theme`) stay choices of the Preferences; a stored choice wins. `d2-theme` in localStorage now keeps `auto` as a word of its own (it was removed before), so the head script can tell "nothing stored" from "follow the system". A host that sets `data-theme` itself and wants to keep it sets `ui.theme=auto`. `theme.js` takes only the words the module knows (`light`, `dark`, `auto`); any other stored text is the default. One-time, accepted: a visitor who had chosen `auto` on the build before this one has nothing stored for it (that build removed the word for `auto`), so the first visit after the change opens light, once; the Preferences then keep `auto` if chosen again. It cannot be told from a first visit.
2. **No status text on the canvas.** The counts ("0 atoms, 0 bonds") are a visually hidden live region (`#d2-counts`, 1 px, clipped, inside `#d2-status` with `role="status"`); the list "What is drawn" of the Check panel says the same. A notice the module keeps (the stereo guard) is still painted at the bottom left, because a sighted person needs it.
3. **About and Report in the Preferences.** The two buttons are the first row of the Preferences flyout (the gear of the File palette): no new always-visible button, reachable by keyboard (the gear, Enter, Tab), touch and pen, 44 px on a finger. The two boxes behave as before; the flyout closes when a box takes the focus and opens again to give the focus back to the opener when a box closes (`feedback.js`).
4. **Panels never overlap.** Cause: `Layout::arrange` wrapped a full column of palettes into a second column that, on a narrow page, no longer fitted beside the first: the final clamp then put the columns on each other (Style over Add a structure, Data over both). Now a column wraps only while the canvas keeps 240 px (`MIN_CANVAS`); otherwise the side panels that do not fit are cut to the room left by a water level of their heights (never below 44 px) and `Rect::capped` says so. The page puts `rect.h` as the panel's `max-height` (the panel scrolls inside itself, with the shadow that says there is more) and measures every side panel uncut, so a panel grows back when the room comes back. The least a cut panel keeps is the line of its title (24 px, `MIN_CUT`). A floating palette that lies over another is moved to the nearest free place beside it (flush against what it overlaps, else the nearest free spot of an 8 px grid) when it is let go; the one in the hand follows the pointer over others, and a keyboard move is let go when the focus leaves the palette (`settle`). So no two palettes overlap, floating ones included. Where there is room (a wide page) the columns wrap as before. The invariant test (`property_no_two_palettes_overlap_and_all_stay_inside...`) walks viewports from 320x320 to 2400x1400 (short landscapes 844x390 and 568x320 included) over the default set, every side group shown, collapsed, dragged to the middle and re-docked on the right edge, with open and collapsed heights; it skips only a layout where even the side groups cut to their title line cannot fit the column, and a floating palette on a viewport narrower than 390 px (a 280 px group beside the 44 px column does not fit in 320 px). Unit tests `crates/mzdraw/tests/palettes.rs` (`side_groups_never_overlap...`, `a_cut_panel_grows_back...`); e2e measures the boxes pairwise at 390x844, 768x1024, 1280x800 and 2000x1130, every side panel open and every one collapsed.
5. **No dotted grip.** The handle of a palette is its frame: a transparent layer behind the buttons (`.grip`, no dots, no room taken, still a button with its name `Move the <name> palette`, its keys and the double click). The empty margin of a palette drags it (4 px, 8 px on a finger); on a side panel the title row drags it too (a click still opens or closes it; a drag does not). Keyboard: Tab to the frame and the arrows (as before), or Alt+arrow (the key is taken, default prevented, only when the module moves the palette, so Alt+Left / Alt+Right are not Back / Forward there; e2e checks that the page is not navigated) from anywhere inside a palette or panel (Shift: 32 px), Alt+Enter docks or floats it, Alt+Escape puts it back; the title row says so in its `aria-description`. Mouse, finger and pen go to the module as before (WCAG 2.5.7: a single pointer with no drag is the arrow keys and Alt+arrows).
6. **Soft change of theme.** While the root carries `d2-fading` (set by `web/fade.js` for 400 ms after a change of the theme attribute or of the system setting) the colours of the page, panels, palettes and flyouts cross-fade over 300 ms (`background-color`, `color`, `border-color`, `fill`, `stroke`, `box-shadow`, `outline-color`). The canvas is painted by script: its old picture is kept as a copy with its old paper over the new one and fades out (`.d2-ghost`), so no white or black flash shows in between. The copy is made before the new theme goes on the root (`applyTheme` calls `fade.before()`; a theme a host sets is copied when seen), so it cannot show the new picture; e2e reads the copy's paper and ink on its first frame. No fade on the first paint (the theme is on the root before it, and the class is not there); with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the switch is instant and no copy is made.

The palettes are the eleven of the general tools palette (`PALETTES`): the general palette has the frame handle and Alt+arrows like the others and is covered by `arrange`; the invariant test takes the count from `PALETTES`. When a floating palette finds no free place beside its neighbours, every floating palette is put again in the first free spot from the top left.

## Tests
- Rust: `palettes.rs` (no overlap at four viewports, open and collapsed; a cut panel grows back), `prefs.rs` (light by default, `dark` and `auto` written and read back, reset gives light); the schema golden has the new default text.
- e2e (`tests/e2e.mjs`, Chromium): the first-visit theme before the first paint on a dark system, a stored `dark` or `auto` kept across a reload; the fade by mouse, keyboard and touch (a sample at about 100 ms is neither colour, every channel stays between the two, the copy of the canvas is half there at some frame, the page settles on the new theme) and instant with reduced motion; the counts hidden; no overlap at four viewports; a panel moved by its title with a mouse, a finger and a pen, by Alt+arrows, and by the margin of a palette; About and Report reached in the Preferences by keyboard, touch (44 px) and pen. Screenshots at deviceScaleFactor 2 in `docs/shots` (`ui-declutter-1-*`: before and after).
- Existing steps keep every assertion. They changed only where the old way no longer exists: the handle is reached by the margin of the palette, the theme steps wait for the fade to end before reading a pixel or taking a screenshot, the dark shots set the theme the way a host does, and the About / Report openers are revealed in the Preferences first.

## Not built
- A drawer or a scroll of the whole column for a phone: a cut panel scrolls inside itself.
- Moving a palette by the title row of a palette of tools (it has none): its margin and the frame handle do it.
- Alt+Escape may be taken by the window manager of some systems; Escape on the focused frame does the same.
