# Format loss manifest, built

(plan-22; `crates/mzdraw/src/loss.rs`, `crates/mzdraw/tests/loss.rs`; section 1c of `docs/mzdraw-formats.md`.) `loss(doc, format)` lists, with ids, the things of one drawing that a format drops or changes; `"out":"loss:FORMAT"` writes it as `loss/1` (`{"format":"loss/1","target","items":[{"what","atoms","bonds","objects"}]}`, one item per thing, ordered by `what` then smallest id, the three lists always written, ascending); `{"lossless":"FORMAT"}` is an arm of `expect` (false when the list is not empty, the `detail` names the kinds, a drawing the format cannot write is false). Formats: `molfile`, `molfile-v3000`, `smiles`, `sdf`, `ket`, `cdxml`, `cml`, `rxn`, `svg`, the document. An unknown format is `error.draw2.request`, an unknown arm value `error.draw2.expect`, a drawing above 50,000 things, above 500 atoms for `smiles` (the SMILES writer of `mzlab-mol` overflows a 1 MiB stack on a chain of 2,000 to 4,000 atoms: refused before it is called), above 600 atoms where the shape is compared (`smiles`, `sdf`, `rxn`) or whose file reads back as more than 3,000 atoms or is refused by its reader, or that a writer would make more than 40,000 atoms of (abbreviations counted as their atoms; the stereo perception of `mzlab-mol` after the read is not linear) where a round trip is needed, `error.draw2.lossSize` (also a false `lossless`, `detail` says too large to check), a drawing a writer refuses its key (`error.draw2.rxnArrow`). No `draw_edit/1` op; one row for the output and one for the arm in the `request` section of the table. No page change.

The table (`loss::TABLE`, rendered in the formats document and compared with it by a test) has a row per feature: abbreviation, annotation, arrow anchor, arrow text, charge, isotope, atom map, radical, data, geometry, the three arrows/objects, R label, unknown fields, the three wedge marks. A cell is `yes`, `no` or `drawing`. A `drawing` cell is decided by writing the file and reading it back, because it depends on the chemistry or the layout: wedge marks (derived again from the stereo, so a mark on a bond that is no centre does not return), the radical in a SMILES, the geometry of a SMILES, an SDF (records side by side) and a reaction file (structures placed about the arrow), the plus signs of a reaction file. For those cells the round trip is the answer, so the property test does not check them independently; the answer is per kind, not per id (the reader does not say which marks went): when some of a kind do not come back, every occurrence of it is listed. The shape is compared only where its cell is `drawing` (`smiles`, `sdf`, `rxn`), as the multiset of distances between the atoms drawn in bond lengths (the median bond length of each drawing is the unit, so a change of scale is not a change of shape), matched within 0.02 of a bond, so a move, a turn-over or atoms added by a reader do not count; the cost is quadratic, hence the atom limits above. For molfile, V3000, KET, CDXML and CML the cell is `yes`: positions are written, the molfile, V3000, KET and CDXML readers rescale to a bond length of 1 and CDXML rounds to a hundredth of a point; none of that is checked or listed, with or without wedge marks (a test with a bond length of 2 gives the same answer either way). Not checked: the element, the bond order, the explicit hydrogen count `h`.

SMILES size: `Document::to_smiles` refuses a drawing above 500 atoms (`error.draw2.smilesSize`), so `out smiles`, `expect smiles`, the page's copy as SMILES and its menu entry, and the stereo guard (which leaves such a piece out) never reach the recursive writer of `mzlab-mol`; `loss` for `smiles` refuses it first with `error.draw2.lossSize`.

Speed: the round trip was quadratic in the atoms (a lookup by id scanned the pool per bond or per atom); `Document::all_hydrogens`, `index_map`, `order_sums`, `layout::pieces`, `duplicate_maps`, `consistent` and the writers' wedge lookups are linear now, with the same output bytes. A 50,000-atom chain with 25,000 wedge marks is listed in about 1 s (release) in every format.

Tests: `crates/mzdraw/tests/loss.rs`. The property writes 500 random drawings (lattice chains of one or two pieces; charges, isotopes, radicals, maps, R labels, abbreviations, wedge marks, data, an annotation, an unknown field, arrows with text and anchors, plus signs, texts) in every format, reads each back and requires the kinds found by `loss::observed` (a comparison by value that does not look at the table) to equal the kinds `loss` lists. Also: an arrow in a molfile, an annotation in SMILES and a wavy bond in V2000 are listed with their ids; the order; a table that forgets the objects of SMILES differs from the round trip; every feature has a row; drawings of the cap with wedge marks, in every probe format, are listed in a bounded time and one above it is refused at once; the SMILES limit holds on a 1 MiB stack. Golden: `tests/data/log/loss.json` (new section `losses`, parity wasm == native, plus `results` and `refused` cases of the arm).

Not built: the warning in the save chooser (arch-std-17 / arch-web-21); enhanced stereo, text runs and highlight (the document has no such feature yet: rows follow when it does); the independent check against the InChI of plan-03 (numbered follow-up in the PR); the explicit hydrogen count `h` of an atom and the scale of a molfile or CDXML (read to a bond length of 1) are not rows of the table.
